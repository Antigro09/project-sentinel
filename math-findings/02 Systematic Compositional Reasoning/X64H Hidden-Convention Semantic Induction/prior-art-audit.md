# Sentinel X64H Hidden-Convention Semantic Induction

## Primary-source prior-art audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-26  
**Status:** research conclusion, not an implementation specification  
**Scope:** primary papers, official proceedings or paper pages, and author-hosted papers/repositories only

## Executive verdict

The X64H direction is mathematically coherent, but its ingredients are not individually novel.

- **Latent communication conventions are not novel.** Prior work models uncertain lexica, partner-specific language models, emergent protocols, and hierarchical partner/community conventions.
- **Bayesian semantic parsing is not novel.** Prior work performs Bayesian inference over CCG derivations, tree transducers, generative semantic grammars, and executable programs.
- **Active information-gain querying is not novel.** Prior work selects clarification questions from language-grounding uncertainty and selects behavioral input/output queries using mutual information.
- **Persistent speaker or partner adaptation is not novel.** Prior work continually adapts a language model to a partner and hierarchically infers partner-specific and community conventions.

The narrow, search-bounded opportunity is the following composition:

> Maintain an executable behavioral version space jointly with a posterior over an episode-persistent latent communication convention; update both with exact verifier evidence; choose both semantic and behavioral clarification questions; and retain explicit probability for expandable out-of-model hypotheses.

No source in this audit directly covers that full composition. This is **not** proof that the composition is globally novel. It is a bounded negative search result. The defensible claim is therefore:

> **Search-bounded novelty delta:** X64H appears to combine previously known mechanisms across semantic parsing, program induction, convention learning, active clarification, and open-world inference in a configuration not directly represented by any source located here. Any publication claim should be framed as a systems-and-formalization contribution and rechecked through a fuller citation graph.

The smallest defensible formalism for the first X64H experiment is a **finite typed synchronous PCFG with an episode-persistent latent convention state**, plus a finite explicit `OTHER` component. It is expressive enough for the specified controlled variation while preserving exact finite enumeration in the deliberately small experimental regime. This is a research conclusion only; it does not authorize implementation in this audit.

## 1. Audit target and comparison vocabulary

Let an X64H episode contain tasks (t=1,\ldots,T) generated under one hidden convention \(\Phi=\phi\). For task \(t\):

- \(Z_t\) is a typed logical form;
- \(B_t\) is an executable behavioral program or continuation-sensitive behavioral class;
- \(U_t\) is an utterance generated by \(p_\phi(u\mid z)\);
- \(D_t\) is behavioral evidence checked by Sentinel's trusted executor/verifier;
- \(H_{<t}\) is prior observed interaction under the same convention;
- \(\bot\) denotes meaning, construction, or behavior outside the in-class model.

The intended posterior is of the form

\[
p(z,\phi\mid u,D,H)
\propto
p(D\mid z)\,p_\phi(u\mid z)\,p(\phi\mid H)\,p(z),
\]

with a behavioral marginal

\[
p(b\mid u,D,H)
=
\sum_{z,\phi}p(b\mid z)\,p(z,\phi\mid u,D,H).
\]

For the direct-coverage test, the following feature codes are used:

| Code | Required X64H component |
|---|---|
| **B** | Explicit executable behavioral version space or posterior over behaviorally admissible programs |
| **C** | A hidden communication convention persistent across multiple tasks/interactions |
| **V** | Execution or verifier feedback that eliminates or scores semantic/program hypotheses |
| **QS** | Agent-selected semantic clarification, such as sense or attachment questions |
| **QB** | Agent-selected behavioral query, such as requesting an output on a chosen input |
| **O** | Explicit open-world mass, expansion, or abstention when the model class is inadequate |

“Exact verifier” means exact relative to the implemented task semantics. It does **not** mean that a surviving program is the intended one: observationally equivalent and spurious programs can remain.

## 2. Search method and limits

The audit searched exact titles and intersecting query families across ACL Anthology, official NeurIPS proceedings, PMLR, ACM SIGPLAN/PLDI, RSS proceedings, OpenReview, arXiv original manuscripts, and author-hosted papers. Discovery-only cross-check queries were also issued against Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, and Google Scholar indexing; claims below rely on the retained primary papers, not index summaries. Query families included combinations of:

- grounded, weakly supervised, executable, and Bayesian semantic parsing;
- CCG, synchronous grammar, tree transducer, latent PCFG, typed grammar, and adaptor grammar;
- hidden, latent, partner-specific, speaker-specific, persistent, and hierarchical convention learning;
- program induction, verifier feedback, clarification, active querying, mutual information, and open-world inference.

Secondary surveys, citation aggregators, blog summaries, and Papers With Code were not used as evidentiary sources. They may have helped expose terminology during search, but every source retained below is an original paper, an official paper page, or an author repository.

This is not an exhaustive systematic review:

- no complete backward-and-forward citation graph was traversed;
- no patent search was performed;
- non-English and non-indexed venues may be underrepresented;
- terminology can hide related work that does not use “convention,” “semantic parsing,” or “clarification”;
- some official pages expose only an abstract, with an official or author PDF used for mechanism details;
- recent preprints can change after this audit;
- the search did not establish priority dates for every possible subsystem composition.

Accordingly, “not found” below means “not found in this bounded primary-source audit,” not “does not exist.”

## 3. Source-by-source audit

### 3.1 Grounded and weakly supervised CCG semantic parsing

#### Source 1

- **Title:** *A Probabilistic Model of Syntactic and Semantic Acquisition from Child-Directed Utterances and their Meanings*
- **Authors:** Tom Kwiatkowski, Sharon Goldwater, Luke Zettlemoyer, Mark Steedman
- **Year:** 2012
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/E12-1024/)
- **Exact mechanism:** An incremental learner receives an utterance paired with a set of contextually possible logical meanings. It jointly infers CCG lexical meanings, syntactic properties, derivations, and a parsing model. Parse structure has a nonparametric Bayesian prior, and training uses online variational Bayesian EM.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It establishes Bayesian joint induction of compositional syntax and semantics under propositional uncertainty, including uncertainty about which contextual meaning is correct.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It has no episode-level hidden speaker convention sampled from a meta-family, no executable behavioral version space, no active semantic/behavioral question policy, and no explicit open-world commitment state.

#### Source 2

- **Title:** *Weakly Supervised Learning of Semantic Parsers for Mapping Instructions to Actions*
- **Authors:** Yoav Artzi, Luke Zettlemoyer
- **Year:** 2013
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/Q13-1005/)
- **Exact mechanism:** A grounded CCG parser learns a joint model of logical meaning and situated context from weak supervision. Visible objects and environment state affect inference; learning can use execution and replicated human actions rather than gold logical forms.
- **Overlap with X64H:** This is close prior art for typed compositional semantics constrained by behavior and situated execution. It directly weakens any claim that executable behavioral supervision for language is new.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The language mapping is not represented as a persistent, sampled convention \(\phi\) shared across a sequence of otherwise distinct tasks. The work does not jointly maintain \(p(z,\phi\mid u,D,H)\), select both semantic and behavioral questions, or reserve expandable `OTHER` mass.

### 3.2 Weakly supervised executable semantic parsing

#### Source 3

- **Title:** *Learning Dependency-Based Compositional Semantics*
- **Authors:** Percy Liang, Michael I. Jordan, Dan Klein
- **Year:** 2013
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/J13-2005/)
- **Exact mechanism:** Dependency-based compositional semantics (DCS) represents latent logical forms as executable tree-structured constraint systems. A log-linear parser is trained from utterance-answer pairs: the logical form is unobserved, and its denotation in a world/database supplies weak supervision.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It is foundational prior art for inducing executable compositional programs from denotational evidence while summing or searching over latent meanings.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It learns a population-level parser, not a posterior over a convention persisting within a speaker episode; it has no persistent convention memory, active clarification policy, or explicit out-of-class state.

#### Source 4

- **Title:** *Semantic Parsing on Freebase from Question-Answer Pairs*
- **Authors:** Jonathan Berant, Andrew Chou, Roy Frostig, Percy Liang
- **Year:** 2013
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/D13-1160/)
- **Exact mechanism:** The parser induces latent executable logical forms from question-answer pairs over Freebase. It constructs candidate logical forms compositionally, induces phrase-predicate associations, and uses bridging operations to connect predicates before execution filters or scores candidates by denotation.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It demonstrates large latent executable search with only behavioral/denotational supervision and exposes the spurious-program problem that exact execution alone cannot solve.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** There is no episode-specific convention variable, no cross-task adaptation to a hidden speaker, no information-gain clarification, and no explicit open-world expansion or calibrated abstention.

#### Source 5

- **Title:** *Grounding Language to Non-Markovian Tasks with No Supervision of Task Specifications*
- **Authors:** Roma Patel, Ellie Pavlick, Stefanie Tellex
- **Year:** 2020
- **Primary URL:** [Robotics: Science and Systems proceedings](https://www.roboticsconference.org/program/papers/16.html)
- **Exact mechanism:** Natural-language instructions and demonstrated trajectories supervise a latent task specification in linear temporal logic. Formal progression of LTL formulas supplies executable task structure without annotated task specifications.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It closely connects language, latent executable temporal programs, and behavioral demonstrations, showing that formal behavior can supervise task-language induction.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It does not infer a persistent speaker convention across multiple distinct tasks, actively ask semantic and behavioral questions, or retain explicit probability for an absent grammar/program.

#### Source 6

- **Title:** *Learning a Natural-Language to LTL Executable Semantic Parser for Grounded Robotics*
- **Authors:** Christopher Wang, Candace Ross, Yen-Ling Kuo, Boris Katz, Andrei Barbu
- **Year:** 2021 (Proceedings of CoRL 2020)
- **Primary URL:** [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v155/wang21g.html)
- **Exact mechanism:** A parser hypothesizes latent LTL formulas from sentence-execution pairs. Candidate meanings are constrained by trajectory permissiveness, scored by a pretrained LTL planner/executor's likelihood of the observed trajectories, and regularized by reconstructing the utterance so meanings do not become overly general.
- **Overlap with X64H:** This is among the closest sources for latent executable semantics plus verifier-like evidence. It explicitly states that the method applies where meanings can be hypothesized and an executor can verify them.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The parser does not expose a posterior over an episode-persistent hidden communication convention, retain and reuse convention evidence across new tasks, choose clarification queries, or use an explicit open-world candidate-expansion/abstention mechanism. An open lexicon is not the same as posterior mass on model inadequacy.

### 3.3 Bayesian program learning

#### Source 7

- **Title:** *Human-Level Concept Learning through Probabilistic Program Induction*
- **Authors:** Brenden M. Lake, Ruslan Salakhutdinov, Joshua B. Tenenbaum
- **Year:** 2015
- **Primary URL:** [Science DOI](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aab3050)
- **Exact mechanism:** Bayesian Program Learning represents concepts as probabilistic generative programs composed from reusable parts, subparts, and relations. Inference compares posterior probabilities of candidate programs and uses the inferred generative structure for one-shot classification and generation.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It establishes Bayesian inference over structured, compositional program hypotheses and hierarchical reuse from sparse evidence.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The observed data are concept exemplars rather than controlled utterances generated under a persistent language convention; there is no executable task verifier, semantic/behavioral clarification, or explicit open-world commitment policy.

#### Source 8

- **Title:** *Sampling for Bayesian Program Learning*
- **Authors:** Kevin Ellis, Armando Solar-Lezama, Joshua B. Tenenbaum
- **Year:** 2016
- **Primary URL:** [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2016/hash/afd4836712c5e77550897e25711e1d96-Abstract.html)
- **Exact mechanism:** Constraint-based program synthesis is combined with randomized parity/XOR constraints to approximately sample programs from a Bayesian posterior rather than return only one satisfying program. The paper supplies approximation guarantees for its sampling construction.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It supports posterior reasoning over multiple programs consistent with examples rather than singleton commitment, closely related to a probabilistic behavioral version space.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It does not model natural-language realization, persistent conventions, cross-task speaker adaptation, clarification questions, or model-class expansion.

### 3.4 DreamCoder and joint language-program models

#### Source 9

- **Title:** *DreamCoder: Bootstrapping Inductive Program Synthesis with Wake-Sleep Library Learning*
- **Authors:** Kevin Ellis, Lionel Wong, Maxwell Nye, Mathias Sablé-Meyer, Lucas Morales, Luke Hewitt, Luc Cary, Armando Solar-Lezama, Joshua B. Tenenbaum
- **Year:** 2021
- **Primary URL:** [Official PLDI paper page](https://pldi21.sigplan.org/details/pldi-2021-papers/55/DreamCoder-Bootstrapping-Inductive-Program-Synthesis-with-Wake-Sleep-Library-Learnin)
- **Exact mechanism:** DreamCoder alternates wake and sleep phases: synthesis solves tasks, e-graph refactoring compresses solved programs into a reusable library, and neural recognition models learn search policies. The library and search policy bootstrap each other through approximate Bayesian learning.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It contributes executable program hypotheses, behavioral examples, reusable abstractions, and persistent learning across a task corpus.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** Core DreamCoder does not infer a hidden speaker convention or semantic parser, and it has no dual semantic/behavioral clarification policy or explicit open-world abstention tied to language-model adequacy.

#### Source 10

- **Title:** *Leveraging Language to Learn Program Abstractions and Search Heuristics*
- **Authors:** Lionel Wong, Kevin M. Ellis, Joshua Tenenbaum, Jacob Andreas
- **Year:** 2021
- **Primary URL:** [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/wong21a.html)
- **Exact mechanism:** LAPS augments DreamCoder with a joint generative language-program model. Natural-language annotations influence which program abstractions enter the learned library and train neurally guided synthesis search; learned abstractions can improve later synthesis even when test tasks lack language hints.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It is direct prior art for joint language-program modeling, executable behavioral tasks, and persistent cross-task transfer.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** Language is auxiliary task annotation rather than an utterance emitted by a sampled convention persistent over an episode. It does not maintain an explicit convention posterior, select semantic and behavioral clarification questions, or allocate open-world mass to missing language constructions/programs.

### 3.5 Adaptor grammars

#### Source 11

- **Title:** *Adaptor Grammars: A Framework for Specifying Compositional Nonparametric Bayesian Models*
- **Authors:** Mark Johnson, Thomas L. Griffiths, Sharon Goldwater
- **Year:** 2006
- **Primary URL:** [NeurIPS proceedings](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2006/hash/62f91ce9b820a491ee78c108636db089-Abstract.html)
- **Exact mechanism:** Adaptor grammars augment PCFG nonterminals with stochastic adaptors so later expansions can depend on previously generated structures. Pitman-Yor adaptors cache and reuse subtrees, and the framework expresses Dirichlet-process and hierarchical-Dirichlet-process language models. The original inference procedure is MCMC.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It provides a principled way to learn recurrent lexical or phrase-level units, share them hierarchically, and expand support nonparametrically—relevant to unknown phrases and convention-specific constructions.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It does not jointly generate typed executable logical forms and behavior, model questions, or define safe commitment. Its nonparametric support is not by itself an explicit `OTHER` posterior, and original posterior inference is approximate rather than exact finite enumeration.

### 3.6 Rational Speech Acts and pragmatic language inference

#### Source 12

- **Title:** *Predicting Pragmatic Reasoning in Language Games*
- **Authors:** Michael C. Frank, Noah D. Goodman
- **Year:** 2012
- **Primary URL:** [Author-hosted paper](https://web.stanford.edu/~ngoodman/papers/FrankGoodman-Science2012.pdf)
- **Exact mechanism:** A Bayesian listener infers a referent by reasoning about a cooperative speaker who selects utterances for informativeness in context. The speaker-listener recursion turns literal semantics and contextual alternatives into pragmatic posterior interpretation.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It establishes Bayesian joint reasoning about utterance meaning and a communication process rather than treating language as a fixed inverse map.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The communication model is not learned as a persistent task-family convention from executable demonstrations, and it has no behavioral version space, active clarification, or open-world expansion.

#### Source 13

- **Title:** *Pragmatic Reasoning through Semantic Inference*
- **Authors:** Leon Bergen, Roger Levy, Noah D. Goodman
- **Year:** 2016
- **Primary URL:** [Semantics and Pragmatics](https://semprag.org/index.php/sp/article/view/sp.9.20)
- **Exact mechanism:** Lexical-uncertainty RSA places uncertainty over lexica or semantic interpretations and performs pragmatic reasoning while integrating over that uncertainty. Observed utterance choice can therefore update beliefs about semantics, not only referents.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It is direct prior art for jointly inferring meaning and uncertain language conventions/lexical semantics.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It does not couple the lexicon posterior to executable task demonstrations across a persistent episode, select semantic and behavioral questions, or represent missing grammar/program support with explicit safe abstention.

#### Source 14

- **Title:** *Program Synthesis with Pragmatic Communication*
- **Authors:** Yewen Pu, Kevin Ellis, Marta Kryven, Joshua B. Tenenbaum, Armando Solar-Lezama
- **Year:** 2020
- **Primary URL:** [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/99c83c904d0d64fbef50d919a5c66a80-Abstract.html)
- **Exact mechanism:** A literal listener reasons over programs consistent with input/output examples; recursive pragmatic speakers and listeners treat chosen examples as intentionally informative. Program enumeration and memoization approximate or compute the recursive communication model over synthesis hypotheses.
- **Overlap with X64H:** This is particularly close to **B + V** and to pragmatic reasoning about demonstrations. It shows that a version space of executable programs can be combined with a communication model.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The messages are selected input/output examples, not controlled-language utterances emitted under a persistent hidden convention. The learner does not adapt a speaker grammar across tasks, choose both semantic and behavioral queries, or reserve expandable out-of-class mass.

### 3.7 Emergent communication and convention learning

#### Source 15

- **Title:** *Emergence of Language with Multi-agent Games: Learning to Communicate with Sequences of Symbols*
- **Authors:** Serhii Havrylov, Ivan Titov
- **Year:** 2017
- **Primary URL:** [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/70222949cc0db89ab32c9969754d4758-Abstract.html)
- **Exact mechanism:** A sender and receiver learn a variable-length discrete communication protocol in referential games. Training compares policy-gradient learning with a straight-through Gumbel-softmax relaxation for discrete messages.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It establishes that a communication convention can be latent, systematic, and learned through interaction rather than authored in advance.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The induced symbols are an end-to-end protocol rather than controlled natural language mapped to typed executable programs; the model lacks an explicit Bayesian convention posterior, exact verifier-driven version space, clarification policy, and open-world state.

#### Source 16

- **Title:** *Continual Adaptation for Efficient Machine Communication*
- **Authors:** Robert Hawkins, Minae Kwon, Dorsa Sadigh, Noah Goodman
- **Year:** 2020
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2020.conll-1.33/)
- **Exact mechanism:** In a repeated reference game, a generic neural speaker/listener is continually and regularizedly fine-tuned to a particular partner. Shared interaction history permits increasingly efficient partner-specific communication while regularization limits destructive drift.
- **Overlap with X64H:** This directly establishes persistent partner adaptation across interactions. Therefore, persistent speaker adaptation cannot be claimed as an X64H novelty by itself.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** Adaptation is implicit in neural parameters rather than an explicit finite posterior over a typed realization convention coupled to executable task meanings. There is no exact verifier, dual query policy, or open-world candidate expansion.

#### Source 17

- **Title:** *Generalizing Meanings from Partners to Populations: Hierarchical Inference Supports Convention Formation on Networks*
- **Authors:** Robert Hawkins, Noah D. Goodman, Adele E. Goldberg, Thomas L. Griffiths
- **Year:** 2020
- **Primary URL:** [Author-hosted proceedings paper](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7849q1dm)
- **Exact mechanism:** A hierarchical Bayesian model represents both partner-specific and community-level mappings in repeated natural-language communication. Evidence from one partner updates local beliefs while population structure supports generalization to other partners.
- **Overlap with X64H:** This is direct prior art for explicit hierarchical inference over persistent partner conventions and a meta-family of conventions.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** Meanings are referential and not typed executable task programs; the model has no verifier-driven behavioral version space, semantic/behavioral active queries, or explicit open-world expansion and abstention.

### 3.8 Active semantic parsing and clarification

#### Source 18

- **Title:** *Toward Information Theoretic Human-Robot Dialog*
- **Authors:** Stefanie Tellex, Pratiksha Thaker, Robin Deits, Thomas Kollar, Nicholas Roy
- **Year:** 2012
- **Primary URL:** [Robotics: Science and Systems proceedings](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss08/p52.html)
- **Exact mechanism:** A robot estimates uncertainty in phrase-to-world grounding, selects a targeted clarification question to reduce that uncertainty, and fuses the original command, the question, and the answer in a joint probabilistic graphical model.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It directly establishes information-theoretic semantic clarification over grounded language. Information-gain clarification is therefore not individually novel.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It does not maintain executable program equivalence classes or a persistent speaker-convention posterior, ask behavioral input/output questions, or expand an explicit open-world grammar/program class.

#### Source 19

- **Title:** *Model-based Interactive Semantic Parsing: A Unified Framework and a Text-to-SQL Case Study*
- **Authors:** Ziyu Yao, Yu Su, Huan Sun, Wen-tau Yih
- **Year:** 2019
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1547/)
- **Exact mechanism:** An interactive parser treats the current SQL parse as agent state, detects where human intervention is needed, generates a natural-language clarification, and uses a world model to update the parse from the initial question and subsequent user feedback.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It combines executable semantic parsing, stateful interaction, and semantic clarification rather than merely returning a one-shot parse.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The interaction corrects an individual parse; it does not infer a persistent sampled convention shared across tasks, maintain an exact behavioral version space, select behavioral test inputs, or retain open-world candidate-expansion mass.

#### Source 20

- **Title:** *Neural Program Synthesis with Query*
- **Authors:** Di Huang, Rui Zhang, Xing Hu, Xishan Zhang, Pengwei Jin, Nan Li, Zidong Du, Qi Guo, Yunji Chen
- **Year:** 2022
- **Primary URL:** [Original arXiv manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.07857)
- **Exact mechanism:** A learned query network selects inputs whose requested outputs are informative about a target program. Query quality is optimized using mutual information between the query/answer and the program, estimated in a differentiable functional representation space.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It is direct prior art for active behavioral clarification via information gain over program hypotheses.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It does not include natural-language semantic questions, persistent speaker conventions, joint \((Z,\Phi)\) inference, exact verifier elimination, or explicit open-world expansion. This source is a preprint, not treated here as evidence of peer-reviewed performance.

### 3.9 Hierarchical Bayesian meta-learning

#### Source 21

- **Title:** *Recasting Gradient-Based Meta-Learning as Hierarchical Bayes*
- **Authors:** Erin Grant, Chelsea Finn, Sergey Levine, Trevor Darrell, Thomas L. Griffiths
- **Year:** 2018
- **Primary URL:** [Official ICLR paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=BJ_UL-k0b)
- **Exact mechanism:** MAML is interpreted as approximate inference in a hierarchical Bayesian model: meta-learned parameters behave as shared hyperparameters or a prior, and task-specific gradient adaptation approximates posterior inference. Curvature-based approximate-inference refinements are derived from this interpretation.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It supplies the general hierarchy “shared meta-family plus rapidly inferred episode-specific latent state,” which can be instantiated with convention parameters.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It does not specify a language convention, typed executable semantics, verifier evidence, clarification, or open-world behavior. Applying hierarchical Bayes to conventions is not enough for an X64H novelty claim.

#### Source 22

- **Title:** *Amortized Bayesian Meta-Learning*
- **Authors:** Sachin Ravi, Alex Beatson
- **Year:** 2019
- **Primary URL:** [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=rkgpy3C5tX)
- **Exact mechanism:** Hierarchical variational inference is amortized across episodes. A learned prior over neural-network weights is adapted with a small number of Bayes-by-Backprop updates to obtain a task-specific approximate posterior with uncertainty estimates.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It is prior art for learned/amortized inference of an episode-specific posterior from a shared meta-prior, matching X64H's proposed exact-versus-amortized inference arms at an abstract level.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It does not define a structured convention variable or connect adaptation to executable semantic hypotheses, active questions, exact verification, or out-of-class expansion.

### 3.10 Latent grammars, synchronous grammars, tree transducers, and typed semantic generation

#### Source 23

- **Title:** *Learning Synchronous Grammars for Semantic Parsing with Lambda Calculus*
- **Authors:** Yuk Wah Wong, Raymond J. Mooney
- **Year:** 2007
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/P07-1121/)
- **Exact mechanism:** A synchronous context-free grammar augmented with lambda operators jointly derives a natural-language string and its logical form. Statistical machine-translation methods learn aligned hierarchical rules from sentence/logical-form pairs.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It is a direct technical foundation for a joint surface/typed-meaning grammar supporting phrase-level alignment and compositional reordering.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The training pairs expose logical forms and the grammar is population-level; there is no episode-persistent hidden convention, behavioral weak supervision, active clarification, or open-world commitment rule.

#### Source 24

- **Title:** *Semantic Parsing with Bayesian Tree Transducers*
- **Authors:** Bevan Jones, Mark Johnson, Sharon Goldwater
- **Year:** 2012
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/P12-1051/)
- **Exact mechanism:** A Bayesian tree-to-string transducer generates natural-language realizations from meaning-representation trees. The studied model uses a weighted root-to-frontier, linear, non-deleting transducer with Bayesian inference over rules.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It provides a probabilistic, compositional realization model in which latent tree-transduction rules can encode lexicalization, phrase realization, and ordering.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The paper does not assign a persistent transducer/convention to each speaker episode, use behavioral demonstrations to identify it, select clarifications, or represent absent semantics/programs. The studied non-deleting restriction also does not directly express omitted semantic arguments.

#### Source 25

- **Title:** *A Probabilistic Generative Grammar for Semantic Parsing*
- **Authors:** Abulhair Saparov, Vijay Saraswat, Tom Mitchell
- **Year:** 2017
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/K17-1026/)
- **Exact mechanism:** A logical form is sampled from a semantic prior, then a probabilistic semantic grammar generates a sentence conditioned on that logical form. Grammar induction uses MCMC on labeled sentence/logical-form pairs; with the learned grammar fixed, the parser exactly finds the highest-posterior logical form under the model.
- **Overlap with X64H:** Its causal direction and joint model—\(z\) first, then \(u\mid z\)—closely match the X64H realization likelihood and support contextual lexical and structural ambiguity.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** It lacks an episode-persistent speaker convention, behavioral/verifier supervision, active semantic and behavioral questions, and explicit out-of-class probability with safe abstention. Exact decoding under a fixed learned grammar is not exact grammar learning.

#### Source 26

- **Title:** *Probabilistic CFG with Latent Annotations*
- **Authors:** Takuya Matsuzaki, Yusuke Miyao, Jun'ichi Tsujii
- **Year:** 2005
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/P05-1010/)
- **Exact mechanism:** Nonterminals are augmented with latent variables, and fine-grained rule refinements are learned from parsed corpora with EM. The paper proves that selecting the most probable unannotated parse after marginalizing latent annotations is NP-hard for the general PCFG-LA model and evaluates approximations.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It provides the technical basis for latent grammar states that can encode contextual lexical sense, local attachment, or convention-conditioned realization choices.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** The latent states are not persistent speaker conventions, and the model is syntactic rather than a joint typed executable semantic grammar with behavioral evidence, questions, or open-world expansion.

#### Source 27

- **Title:** *On the Complexity of CCG Parsing*
- **Authors:** Marco Kuhlmann, Giorgio Satta, Peter Jonsson
- **Year:** 2018
- **Primary URL:** [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/J18-3004/)
- **Exact mechanism:** A formal complexity analysis of Vijay-Shanker–Weir CCG recognition. Universal recognition is NP-complete for a restricted lexicalized class and EXPTIME-complete for unrestricted VW-CCG when grammar size is part of the input.
- **Overlap with X64H:** It does not propose an induction mechanism, but it supplies a necessary warning against describing unrestricted CCG inference as cheaply exact.
- **Narrow remaining novelty delta:** X64H could still use a deliberately restricted finite CCG, but the source gives no convention-learning, verifier, query, or open-world mechanism. Its role here is to constrain the formalism recommendation.

### Direct-coverage determination

No audited source has **B + C + V + QS + QB + O**. More importantly, no source found jointly performs all of the following in one probabilistic state:

1. preserves multiple executable behavioral hypotheses rather than collapsing to one parse;
2. treats the speaker's realization system as a latent variable persistent across tasks;
3. updates meaning and convention beliefs with exact trusted-executor evidence;
4. asks either a semantic question or a deliberately chosen behavioral test according to one decision objective;
5. allows the semantic grammar, realization grammar, or behavioral pool to be inadequate and routes that probability to expansion or abstention.

This supports only a **provisional composition-level novelty hypothesis**. It does not support claims that latent conventions, Bayesian semantic parsing, active information gain, executable weak supervision, or persistent adaptation are new.

## 5. Candidate formalism comparison

### 5.1 Comparison criteria

The first X64H model needs to express:

- synonym selection;
- contextual polysemy;
- word-order conventions;
- phrase-level realization;
- optional function words;
- omitted arguments;
- local attachment choices;
- one convention persisting over a sequence of tasks;
- typed executable logical forms;
- exact inference in a finite falsification experiment, at least by enumeration.

“Identifiable” below always means identifiable only modulo observational symmetries. Renaming latent nonterminals, semantic atoms, or transducer states without changing any observable distribution cannot be identified from data. X64H should score posterior mass on the correct observational equivalence class, not require recovery of an arbitrary internal name.

### 5.2 Comparative table

| Formalism | Expressivity for X64H | Identifiability | Exact-inference profile | Research conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Probabilistic tree transducer** | Strong for tree-to-string lexicalization, phrase templates, reordering, and state-conditioned realization. Deletion is needed for omitted semantic material; the Jones–Johnson–Goldwater model's linear non-deleting restriction does not supply it. | Only up to state/rule and semantic-tree automorphisms unless anchors or separating contexts are imposed. Rich transducer states can trade off against lexical rules, creating additional non-identifiability. | Dynamic programming is available for restricted transducer classes and fixed structures, but Bayesian rule learning in the cited model is approximate. General transducers do not provide a blanket cheap exact posterior. | Expressive, but more machinery than the smallest controlled test needs; deletion and inference restrictions must be stated explicitly. |
| **Probabilistic synchronous grammar** | Naturally co-generates surface and logical-form trees. Hierarchical rules express phrases, local reordering, lexical alternatives, and attachment; epsilon/nullable surface components express optional words and omission if tightly restricted. | Identifiable only up to synchronous nonterminal/rule and semantic automorphisms. Separating behavioral contexts or anchored productions are needed to distinguish conventions. | For a fixed finite, binarized grammar and bounded semantic candidate set, inside computation is polynomial in utterance length. Exact joint inference becomes expensive in grammar size, number of conventions, and semantic candidates; unrestricted grammar induction remains hard/approximate. | Best structural basis for the first finite model. |
| **CCG-style lexicon** | Excellent type discipline and compositional semantics; lexical ambiguity handles synonyms/polysemy, and combinators handle flexible composition. Ellipsis/omission often requires empty entries or extra combinators. | Lexical entries, derivations, type raising, and composition can be observationally equivalent. | **Adaptor grammar** | Best of the candidates for reusable, open-ended words and phrases through Pitman-Yor cached subtrees. ### 5.3 Exact finite inference envelope

Let:

- \(n=|u|\) be utterance length;
- \(|\Phi|\) be the finite number of convention hypotheses retained;
- \(|\mathcal Z_D|\) be the number of typed logical forms consistent enough with behavioral evidence to score;
- \(|G|\) be the size of a fixed binarized surface grammar;
- \(|N|\) be its number of chart nonterminals.

If each \((\phi,z)\) induces a fixed weighted binarized CFG over the surface realization and derivations are marginalized with an inside algorithm, a conservative explicit-enumeration bound is

\[
O\!\left(|\Phi|\,|\mathcal Z_D|\,|G|\,n^3\right)
\]

time and

\[
O\!\left(|\Phi|\,|\mathcal Z_D|\,|N|\,n^2\right)
\]

memory if charts are retained for all pairs, or proportionally less memory if pairs are streamed. These are **derived design bounds**, not general complexity claims about synchronous grammars. They depend on:

1. finite enumerated \(\Phi\) and \(\mathcal Z_D\);
2. bounded-arity typed semantic constructors;
3. a binarized grammar without unrestricted cyclic epsilon rules;
4. fixed grammar parameters during episode inference;
5. no search over arbitrary new grammar topology inside the exact loop.

If any of these restrictions is removed, candidate generation, grammar induction, or marginal-MAP decoding can dominate and become exponential or NP-hard. This distinction must remain visible in X64H claims: **exact episode inference in a frozen finite model is plausible; exact general grammar induction is not established.**

### 5.4 Recommended smallest formal family

Use a **finite typed synchronous PCFG with a persistent finite convention state** (FT-SPCFG):

\[
\phi \sim p_\eta(\phi),\qquad
z_t \sim p(z),\qquad
(u_t,d_t) \sim G_\phi(\cdot,\cdot\mid z_t),
\]

where \(d_t\) is a latent synchronous derivation and each grammar rule has:

1. a typed semantic constructor or semantic child alignment;
2. a surface expansion;
3. a probability conditioned on \(\phi\).

The convention state should initially be factored into a small finite tuple rather than an arbitrary grammar:

\[
\phi=(\pi_{\mathrm{lex}},\rho_{\mathrm{order}},\sigma_{\mathrm{sense}},
\tau_{\mathrm{phrase}},\omega_{\mathrm{optional}},
\kappa_{\mathrm{drop}},\alpha_{\mathrm{attach}}).
\]

This tuple directly corresponds to the seven required sources of systematic variation. Persistence comes from holding the tuple fixed for an episode, not from independently corrupting each utterance.

For the first open-world arm, add a finite `OTHER` mixture component with separately calibrated likelihoods for unknown lexical item, unknown construction, absent semantic form, and absent behavioral program. Do not begin with an adaptor grammar or Pitman-Yor grammar expansion unless the finite `OTHER` experiment first shows that explicit model-inadequacy mass is useful and calibratable.

## 6. What is and is not a defensible novelty claim

### Not novel individually

The following claims should be prohibited in X64H documentation:

- “X64H introduces latent language conventions.”
- “X64H introduces Bayesian semantic parsing.”
- “X64H introduces information-gain clarification.”
- “X64H introduces adaptation to a persistent speaker.”
- “X64H is the first to combine language and executable programs.”
- “X64H is the first to use behavioral demonstrations to induce formal task semantics.”
- “X64H is the first open-world grammar model.”

Primary sources above directly contradict each of those ingredient-level framings.

### Potentially defensible after experiment and expanded search

A narrower statement may become defensible:

> X64H evaluates a unified finite Bayesian mechanism that jointly induces typed task meaning and a persistent hidden realization convention from controlled language, exact verifier evidence, and actively selected semantic or behavioral clarifications, while retaining explicit mass for out-of-model meanings, constructions, and programs.

Even this should be presented as a **specific integration and evaluation contribution**, not as a new general theory of language learning. Its value would depend on measured results that separate convention learning from task repetition and rule out leakage from the convention generator.

## 7. Strongest prior-art objections

1. **“This is hierarchical Bayesian convention learning plus semantic parsing.”** Correct at the ingredient level. Hawkins et al. already supply partner/population convention inference, while Kwiatkowski et al., Jones et al., and Saparov et al. supply Bayesian semantic grammars. X64H must justify the executable/verifier/query/open-world integration empirically.
2. **“This is grounded weakly supervised parsing with a speaker ID.”** Artzi–Zettlemoyer and the LTL work are close. X64H differs only if \(\phi\) is a genuinely hidden structured cause that transfers across new task compositions, not a nuisance embedding or task identifier.
3. **“This is RSA over programs.”** Pu et al. already combine pragmatic communication and executable program version spaces. X64H's remaining distinction is controlled-language realization under a persistent latent convention plus active dual-mode queries and open-world expansion.
4. **“This is interactive semantic parsing.”** Tellex et al. and Yao et al. already ask targeted semantic questions, while Huang et al. actively chooses behavioral examples. X64H must show benefit from optimizing questions over the joint \((Z,\Phi)\) posterior and from choosing between question types.
5. **“The open-world component is only a reject option.”** That objection is valid unless X64H separately measures unknown detection, calibrated `OTHER` mass, confirmation/expansion behavior, and false confident in-class answers.

## 8. Audit conclusion

**Direct verdict:** The hidden-convention direction is well grounded in existing mathematics and addresses the known-realizer flaw in X64G, but it is not a new primitive. Its credible research contribution is the controlled, falsifiable synthesis of established components under a leak-resistant freeze protocol.

**Direct-coverage result:** Within the stated search limits, no primary source was found that directly combines all six audited components **B + C + V + QS + QB + O**. The closest pairwise and three-way overlaps are:

- executable weak supervision: Artzi–Zettlemoyer; Patel–Pavlick–Tellex; Wang et al.;
- executable version spaces plus pragmatic communication: Pu et al.;
- joint language-program learning: LAPS;
- persistent and hierarchical convention learning: Hawkins et al.;
- semantic information-gain clarification: Tellex et al.;
- interactive executable parse repair: Yao et al.;
- behavioral information-gain queries: Huang et al.;
- nonparametric phrase reuse/expansion: adaptor grammars.

**Formalism result:** A finite typed synchronous PCFG with an explicit episode-level convention tuple and finite `OTHER` component is the smallest defensible first model. Tree transducers and CCG are viable but add complexity; adaptor grammars are a plausible later expansion mechanism but sacrifice the clean exact finite inference regime needed for X64H's first decisive test.

**Claim discipline:** Until X64H passes its preregistered hidden-convention, reset, active-query, conflict, and open-world gates, the composition-level novelty remains a hypothesis, not a demonstrated contribution.

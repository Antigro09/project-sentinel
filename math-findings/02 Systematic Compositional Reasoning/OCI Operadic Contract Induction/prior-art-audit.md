# OCI — Operadic Contract Induction

## Bounded primary-source prior-art and novelty audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-26  
**Status:** Research conclusion, not an implementation specification  
**Scope:** Primary papers, official proceedings/publisher pages, and author repositories only. This is a bounded audit, not an exhaustive novelty or patent search.

## Executive verdict

OCI is mathematically coherent, but most of its ingredients are established prior art.

The following are **clearly not novel**:

- describing typed constructors by operations, generators, and equations;
- interpreting syntax compositionally through a homomorphism or functor into executable semantics;
- using colored operads and their algebras to compose typed open systems;
- treating operadic equations as interface-like contracts;
- quotienting terms by equations and seeking terminating, confluent rewrite systems or canonical normal forms;
- inferring rewrite rules from an interpreter and validating them with testing or solvers;
- representing compositional structure with tensors or categorical maps;
- assembling reusable neural or symbolic modules into unseen trees;
- using active queries and distinguishing contexts to identify latent states or grammars;
- enforcing equivariance or identifying hypotheses only up to symmetry;
- attaching machine-checkable proofs or contracts to executable artifacts.

The closest direct collision is Foley, Breiner, Subrahmanian, and Dusel (2021): it uses operads for system design, treats equations as additional contracts on an API-like compositional syntax, and explicitly discusses normal-form computation. Bottman and Richardson (2026) additionally apply an operad/algebra formulation directly to compositional reasoning in language models and test consistency across different evaluations of a decomposition tree. Nandi et al. (2021) infer rewrite rules from an interpreter with equality saturation and domain-specific validation. Angluin-style active learning uses queries and separating suffixes or contexts to identify an unknown behavior. These sources substantially narrow any novelty claim.

No source in the bounded corpus directly covered the **full OCI loop**:

1. a typed/colored operad whose generators are executable semantic constructors;
2. homomorphic execution of unseen composition trees;
3. persistent induction of new quotient equations, admitted only after an explicitly scoped verifier certificate;
4. canonical normalization only when termination and confluence have actually been established;
5. active intervention contexts that identify a new *surface symbol* with a generator only up to the automorphism group left observationally indistinguishable; and
6. retention of that certified symbol-to-generator contract for transfer to later compositions.

That conjunction has a **defensible provisional novelty delta**, but only as a systems-and-learning composition. It is not evidence that any underlying algebraic construction is new. The strongest defensible formulation is:

> **Provisional novelty hypothesis:** OCI may be a new integration of executable colored-operad semantics, verifier-gated quotient induction, conditional canonicalization, and active symmetry-aware surface-symbol identification for systematic transfer.

This must remain provisional until a broader citation-chain audit and a working experiment establish that every listed component is operational rather than merely terminological.

## 1. Audited mathematical target

Let $C$ be a finite set of semantic types (colors). For each profile

\[
(c_1,\ldots,c_n;c),
\]

let \(\Sigma(c_1,\ldots,c_n;c)\) be a set of primitive semantic constructors. The free $C$-colored operad \(F_C(\Sigma)\) contains the well-typed composition trees generated by \(\Sigma\), identities, and—if the symmetric version is chosen—input permutations.

For typed state spaces \((X_c)_{c\in C}\), define the endomorphism operad

\[
\operatorname{End}_X(c_1,\ldots,c_n;c)
=
\operatorname{Hom}(X_{c_1}\times\cdots\times X_{c_n},X_c).
\]

OCI's executable semantics is an operad algebra

\[
\alpha:F_C(\Sigma)\to \operatorname{End}_X.
\]

Thus, for any tree \(t=g(t_1,\ldots,t_n)\),

\[
\alpha(t)=\alpha(g)\bigl(\alpha(t_1),\ldots,\alpha(t_n)\bigr).
\]

This gives exact generalization to an unseen tree **by construction**, conditional on four assumptions: the tree is in the generated typed language; its primitive generators have correct semantics; the implementation of \(\alpha\) respects operad composition; and the desired behavior is extensional in the selected state spaces. It does not prove that a parser will recover the right tree, that omitted primitives will be discovered, or that execution will generalize beyond the declared algebra.

Let \(E\) be a set of certified same-profile equations \(l=r\). Its compatible closure \(\equiv_E\) is an operadic congruence, and the semantic syntax becomes

\[
\mathcal O_E=F_C(\Sigma)/{\equiv_E}.
\]

An oriented system \(R\) derived from \(E\) yields a canonical normal form only if termination and confluence are established on the intended term language. If either property is unknown, OCI may maintain a congruence or e-graph, but it must not claim unique normal forms.

For a generator \(g\) and a family of typed one-hole intervention contexts \(P=\{p_1,\ldots,p_m\}\), define a boundary signature

\[
\sigma_P(g)
=
\bigl(V(\alpha(p_1[g])),\ldots,V(\alpha(p_m[g]))\bigr),
\]

where \(V\) is the declared verifier observation: output, trace projection, postcondition vector, or another contract-relevant observable. If a symmetry group \(G\) acts on generators without changing all available observations, identification can be no finer than the orbit \([g]_G\). A sound transfer gate therefore requires

\[
\sigma_P(g)=\sigma_P(h)\Longrightarrow h\in[g]_G
\]

over the admitted candidate set. This is a separating-family condition, not a general theorem that a finite separating family always exists.

### Certification scope warning

“Verifier-certified equation” must be qualified. Exhaustive execution on a finite semantic domain certifies only that finite model. Random tests, unit tests, or bounded probes provide evidence, not a universal proof. A universal equation requires, for example, a proof in a sound logic, an SMT proof under explicit theories and assumptions, or a complete finite-model argument. OCI should store the certificate type, assumptions, observation boundary, and domain with every learned equation.

## 2. Algebraic and functorial semantics

### 2.1 Lawvere theories

**Source:** F. William Lawvere, **“Functorial Semantics of Algebraic Theories”** (1963; 2004 reprint). [Official TAC paper page](https://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/reprints/articles/5/tr5abs.html); [paper PDF](https://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/reprints/articles/5/tr5.pdf).

- **Exact mechanism:** An algebraic theory is represented categorically; models are structure-preserving functors into a semantic category. Operations and equations are separated from their realizations.
- **Overlap with OCI:** OCI's generator/equation presentation and semantics-preserving map are instances of the same algebraic-semantics pattern, specialized to many-input/one-output typed composition.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** Lawvere does not learn surface symbols, actively identify generators, retain verifier evidence, or induce equations from interaction.

### 2.2 Initial-algebra semantics

**Source:** Joseph A. Goguen, James W. Thatcher, Eric G. Wagner, and Jesse B. Wright, **“Initial Algebra Semantics and Continuous Algebras”** (1977). [DOI: 10.1145/321992.321997](https://doi.org/10.1145/321992.321997).

- **Exact mechanism:** Syntax generated by a signature is treated as an initial algebra; a semantic interpretation is the unique homomorphism into a target algebra satisfying the primitive interpretations.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Homomorphic extension from primitive constructors to arbitrary unseen terms is foundational initial-algebra semantics. This aspect of OCI is not a learned generalization theorem.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI proposes to combine the fixed homomorphic extension with learned, certified equations and actively induced language bindings.

### 2.3 Categorical architecture constraints

**Source:** Bruno Gavranović, Paul Lessard, Andrew Dudzik, Tamara von Glehn, João G. M. Araújo, and Petar Veličković, **“Position: Categorical Deep Learning is an Algebraic Theory of All Architectures”** (2024). [arXiv:2402.15332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15332).

- **Exact mechanism:** Uses universal algebra in a 2-category of parametric maps to connect architectural constraints with trainable implementations and to recover standard neural and automata constructions.
- **Overlap with OCI:** It directly weakens any claim that algebraic theories, functorial constraints, or compositional architectures are novel in machine learning.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** It does not present OCI's verifier-gated equation induction or active surface-generator contract gate.

## 3. Operads for typed system and reasoning composition

### 3.1 Wiring-diagram operads

**Source:** David I. Spivak, **“The operad of wiring diagrams: formalizing a graphical language for databases, recursion, and plug-and-play circuits”** (2013). [arXiv:1305.0297](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0297).

- **Exact mechanism:** Defines an operad whose operations are wiring diagrams; operad algebras assign compositional semantics to database, circuit, and recursive-system constructions.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Typed ports, many-input/one-output substitution, and separation of compositional syntax from semantic realization are direct foundations for OCI.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** The paper does not infer language conventions, learn equations from a verifier, or use probes to identify unknown generators.

**Source:** Dmitry Vagner, David I. Spivak, and Eugene Lerman, **“Algebras of Open Dynamical Systems on the Operad of Wiring Diagrams”** (2015). [arXiv:1408.1598](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1598).

- **Exact mechanism:** Constructs algebras assigning open dynamical systems to wiring-diagram types, so large systems inherit semantics by operadic composition.
- **Overlap with OCI:** This is close prior art for executable semantics as an algebra over an operad of typed compositions.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** Its generators and semantics are modeled, not induced from surface evidence through an exact verifier.

### 3.2 Operads as contracts, equations, and normal-form computations

**Source:** John D. Foley, Spencer Breiner, Eswaran Subrahmanian, and John M. Dusel, **“Operads for complex system design specification, analysis and synthesis”** (2021). [DOI: 10.1098/rspa.2021.0099](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2021.0099); [arXiv:2101.11115](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.11115).

- **Exact mechanism:** Represents complex-system design by operadic syntax and multiple semantic algebras; uses type discipline for legal composition, equations to constrain implementations, algebra homomorphisms to move between analyses, and normal-form computations where a semantic algebra permits them.
- **Overlap with OCI:** This is the strongest collision with the phrase “operadic contract.” The source explicitly treats equations between operations as additional API contracts and discusses deciding equality by normal-form computation. Operadic interfaces, semantic implementations, equations-as-contracts, and normal-form composition are therefore not OCI novelties.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** The paper does not learn those contracts from Sentinel's executable verifier, does not condition their status on proof artifacts, and does not actively identify a novel surface symbol up to observational symmetry for persistent transfer.

### 3.3 Operads for compositional LLM reasoning

**Source:** Nathaniel Bottman and Kyle Richardson, **“Operads for compositional reasoning in LLMs”** (2026). [arXiv:2606.13634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13634).

- **Exact mechanism:** Defines a questions operad in which question templates compose by substitution and interprets question-answering systems as algebras. Different partial collapses of a decomposition tree should agree under an operadic consistency condition.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Operads applied specifically to compositional language reasoning, tree decomposition, and consistency across composite evaluations are now direct prior art.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** It diagnoses consistency of an existing QA model; it does not induce semantic generators from novel symbols, synthesize a certified quotient theory, or learn persistent contracts through interventions.

**Source:** Nathaniel Bottman, Yinhong Liu, and Kyle Richardson, **“Operadic consistency: a label-free signal for compositional reasoning failures in LLMs”** (2026). [arXiv:2606.13649](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13649).

- **Exact mechanism:** Compares direct and decomposed answers as an inference-time operadic-consistency signal and evaluates that signal across models and multi-hop QA datasets.
- **Overlap with OCI:** It anticipates verifier-like comparison of different evaluations of a composition tree, although the comparison is an empirical consistency signal rather than a proof of semantic equality.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI would use an authoritative executable verifier to admit or reject persistent algebraic contracts and symbol mappings, rather than use consistency only as a confidence correlate.

## 4. Quotient equations, rewriting, and certified normalization

### 4.1 Completion and confluence

**Source:** Donald E. Knuth and Peter B. Bendix, **“Simple Word Problems in Universal Algebras”** (1970). [DOI: 10.1016/B978-0-08-012975-4.50028-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-08-012975-4.50028-X).

- **Exact mechanism:** Orients equations into rewrite rules and resolves critical pairs in an attempt to construct a complete rewriting system for an equational theory.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Converting certified equations into a terminating/confluent normalization procedure is classical completion, not a new OCI device.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI's equations would be acquired from semantic interaction and carry verifier provenance; completion itself remains standard and may fail or diverge.

**Source:** Gérard Huet, **“Confluent Reductions: Abstract Properties and Applications to Term Rewriting Systems”** (1980). [DOI: 10.1145/322217.322230](https://doi.org/10.1145/322217.322230).

- **Exact mechanism:** Establishes foundational relations among reduction, termination, local confluence, and confluence for term-rewriting systems.
- **Overlap with OCI:** The conditions supporting unique normal forms are established rewriting theory.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI must apply these conditions to a typed operadic rewrite relation and must not silently infer global confluence from successful finite tests.

### 4.2 Equality saturation and learned rewrite systems

**Source:** Max Willsey, Chandrakana Nandi, Yisu Remy Wang, Oliver Flatt, Zachary Tatlock, and Pavel Panchekha, **“egg: Fast and Extensible Equality Saturation”** (2021). [DOI: 10.1145/3434304](https://doi.org/10.1145/3434304); [arXiv:2004.03082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03082); [author repository](https://github.com/egraphs-good/egg).

- **Exact mechanism:** E-graphs compactly maintain congruence classes while equality saturation applies many rewrites without committing to a destructive orientation; extraction chooses a representative by cost.
- **Overlap with OCI:** It is a strong fallback when OCI has sound equations but lacks a terminating/confluent orientation.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** Equality saturation neither proves candidate equations nor supplies generator-identifying probes. A cheapest extracted term is not automatically a canonical normal form of the equational theory.

**Source:** Chandrakana Nandi, Max Willsey, Amy Zhu, Yisu Remy Wang, Brett Saiki, Adam Anderson, Adriana Schulz, Dan Grossman, and Zachary Tatlock, **“Rewrite Rule Inference Using Equality Saturation”** (2021). [DOI: 10.1145/3485496](https://doi.org/10.1145/3485496); [arXiv:2108.10436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10436); [author repository](https://github.com/uwplse/ruler).

- **Exact mechanism:** Ruler enumerates terms from a grammar, uses e-graphs to collapse known equivalences, proposes compact rewrite rules, and validates candidates with a domain-selected technique such as fuzzing or an SMT-backed procedure.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Automatically inferring rewrite rules from executable semantics and validating them is direct prior art. OCI cannot claim “the system learns equations from a verifier” as novel in isolation.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI's possible difference is the joint restriction to same-profile operadic equations, explicit proof-scope metadata, active surface-generator identification, and persistent use of the resulting quotient for semantic transfer. Fuzzing-based acceptance would not meet a universal “certified” claim.

**Source:** Jiangyi Liu, Charlie Murphy, Anvay Grover, Keith J. C. Johnson, Thomas Reps, and Loris D'Antoni, **“Synthesizing Formal Semantics from Executable Interpreters”** (2024). [arXiv:2408.14668](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14668).

- **Exact mechanism:** Synantic uses counterexample-guided synthesis to infer syntax-directed formal semantics, represented as constrained Horn clauses, from a grammar and a closed-box executable interpreter.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Inducing compositional semantic rules from an executable oracle is already a demonstrated program-synthesis problem.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** Synantic targets formal-semantics synthesis, not a colored-operad quotient learned jointly with symbol contracts and active symmetry-separating probes.

## 5. Tensor and categorical compositional representations

### 5.1 Tensor-product representations

**Source:** Paul Smolensky, **“Tensor Product Variable Binding and the Representation of Symbolic Structures in Connectionist Systems”** (1990). [DOI: 10.1016/0004-3702(90)90007-M](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(90)90007-M).

- **Exact mechanism:** Encodes role–filler bindings using tensor products and recursively builds distributed representations of symbolic structures.
- **Overlap with OCI:** It provides a principled compositional representation for trees and bindings and is an obvious alternative to explicit symbolic operad terms.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** A representation of structure is not an executable operad algebra, a quotient proof system, or an active identification criterion. Exact full tensors grow rapidly with arity/depth; compressed variants trade exact recovery for interference.

**Source:** Tony A. Plate, **“Holographic Reduced Representations”** (1995). [DOI: 10.1109/72.377968](https://doi.org/10.1109/72.377968); [PubMed paper page](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18263348/).

- **Exact mechanism:** Uses circular convolution and correlation to compress role–filler compositions into fixed-width vectors with approximate retrieval.
- **Overlap with OCI:** HRR is a candidate “tensor-signature” substrate for compact boundary signatures.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** Approximate superposition introduces collisions and retrieval noise, conflicting with exact equation certification and symmetry-sensitive generator identification unless a separate symbolic authority remains in charge.

### 5.2 Categorical compositional distributional semantics

**Source:** Bob Coecke, Mehrnoosh Sadrzadeh, and Stephen Clark, **“Mathematical Foundations for a Compositional Distributional Model of Meaning”** (2010). [arXiv:1003.4394](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.4394); [author-hosted paper](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/files/2879/LambekFestPlain.pdf).

- **Exact mechanism:** Maps grammatical reductions in a compact closed category to linear maps that compose tensor-valued word meanings into a sentence meaning.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Functorial composition of typed linguistic structure into semantic operations is direct prior art.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** DisCoCat composes distributional vectors; it does not provide OCI's authoritative executable contracts, certified rewrite quotient, or active symbol-identification boundary.

## 6. Neural modules and executable compositional programs

### 6.1 Neural module networks

**Source:** Jacob Andreas, Marcus Rohrbach, Trevor Darrell, and Dan Klein, **“Neural Module Networks”** (2016). [Official CVPR paper page](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2016/html/Andreas_Neural_Module_Networks_CVPR_2016_paper.html); [arXiv:1511.02799](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.02799).

- **Exact mechanism:** Dynamically assembles reusable neural modules according to linguistic substructure and trains the modules jointly for visual question answering.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Typed or role-specific modules composed into a new computation graph are not new.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** NMNs generally learn approximate modules and layouts without a formal quotient theory, proof-scoped contracts, or active generator identification up to automorphism.

**Source:** Justin Johnson, Bharath Hariharan, Laurens van der Maaten, Judy Hoffman, Li Fei-Fei, C. Lawrence Zitnick, and Ross Girshick, **“Inferring and Executing Programs for Visual Reasoning”** (2017). [Official ICCV paper page](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2017/html/Johnson_Inferring_and_Executing_ICCV_2017_paper.html); [arXiv:1705.03633](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.03633).

- **Exact mechanism:** A program generator maps language to explicit functional programs that a neural execution engine runs compositionally on CLEVR scenes.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Language-to-program induction plus compositional execution is direct prior art.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** The system does not induce a certified equational quotient or require intervention signatures before persistent symbol transfer.

**Source:** Nasim Rahaman, Muhammad Waleed Gondal, Shruti Joshi, Peter Gehler, Yoshua Bengio, Francesco Locatello, and Bernhard Schölkopf, **“Dynamic Inference with Neural Interpreters”** (2021/2022). [arXiv:2110.06399](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06399).

- **Exact mechanism:** Learns reusable functions and type/signature-based routing so that modules can be dynamically selected and composed; evaluates systematic generalization and capacity extension.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Function signatures, modular reuse, and dynamic composition overlap strongly at the architectural level.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** Learned signatures are continuous routing devices, not exact observational separating signatures backed by a verifier and quotient proof objects.

## 7. Compositional-generalization benchmarks

### 7.1 SCAN

**Source:** Brenden M. Lake and Marco Baroni, **“Generalization without Systematicity: On the Compositional Skills of Sequence-to-Sequence Recurrent Networks”** (2018). [Official PMLR paper page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/lake18a.html); [arXiv:1711.00350](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00350).

- **Exact mechanism:** SCAN maps synthetic commands to action sequences under splits that expose failures to recombine familiar primitives systematically.
- **Overlap with OCI:** It motivates held-out composition-tree testing.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** SCAN is a benchmark and diagnosis, not a contract-learning mechanism; its finite command grammar does not test learned quotient equations or active symbol identification.

### 7.2 COGS

**Source:** Najoung Kim and Tal Linzen, **“COGS: A Compositional Generalization Challenge Based on Semantic Interpretation”** (2020). [Official ACL Anthology page](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.731/); [DOI: 10.18653/v1/2020.emnlp-main.731](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2020.emnlp-main.731).

- **Exact mechanism:** Tests semantic parsing under systematic distribution gaps involving lexical roles, argument structure, and structural depth.
- **Overlap with OCI:** It is a relevant control for whether a claimed mechanism transfers beyond memorized surface templates.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** COGS does not test active interventions, certified equations, or persistent symbol contracts.

### 7.3 CFQ

**Source:** Daniel Keysers et al., **“Measuring Compositional Generalization: A Comprehensive Method on Realistic Data”** (2020). [arXiv:1912.09713](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09713).

- **Exact mechanism:** Introduces CFQ and distribution splits based on atom and compound divergence to test recombination in semantic parsing.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Compound-divergence controls are useful for separating true composition transfer from familiar local patterns.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** CFQ evaluates input-to-query models; it does not validate the operadic algebra, verifier certificates, equation completion, or symbol-probe gate.

### Benchmark implication

Passing SCAN, COGS, or CFQ would not prove OCI's claimed mechanism. A decisive OCI test must separately hold out:

- tree shapes and depths;
- generator combinations;
- equations and normal-form paths;
- surface symbols mapped to otherwise familiar generators;
- whole generator orbits under declared symmetries; and
- probe contexts needed to distinguish those orbits.

## 8. Neural and symbolic program induction

### 8.1 Search guided by learned program properties

**Source:** Matej Balog, Alexander L. Gaunt, Marc Brockschmidt, Sebastian Nowozin, and Daniel Tarlow, **“DeepCoder: Learning to Write Programs”** (2017). [arXiv:1611.01989](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01989); [official Microsoft Research page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/deepcoder-learning-write-programs/).

- **Exact mechanism:** Predicts which DSL primitives are likely to occur and uses those predictions to guide enumerative or solver-based program synthesis from input-output examples.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Identifying semantic constructors from behavioral evidence is part of established program induction.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** DeepCoder does not impose an operadic quotient, learn verified equations among programs, or require an orbit-separating intervention signature before lexical transfer.

### 8.2 Learned libraries and e-graph refactoring

**Source:** Kevin Ellis, Catherine Wong, Maxwell Nye, Mathias Sablé-Meyer, Lucas Morales, Luke B. Hewitt, Luc Cary, Armando Solar-Lezama, and Joshua B. Tenenbaum, **“DreamCoder: Bootstrapping Inductive Program Synthesis with Wake-Sleep Library Learning”** (2021). [Official PLDI paper page](https://pldi21.sigplan.org/details/pldi-2021-papers/55/DreamCoder-Bootstrapping-Inductive-Program-Synthesis-with-Wake-Sleep-Library-Learnin); [arXiv:2006.08381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381); [author repository](https://github.com/ellisk42/ec).

- **Exact mechanism:** Alternates program search, learned recognition, and library abstraction; refactors solution programs to invent reusable DSL primitives.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Persistent abstraction and transfer through learned reusable program components are direct prior art.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI would focus on learning proof-scoped equations and lexical contracts over a typed operad rather than inventing a probabilistic DSL library through wake–sleep compression.

## 9. Equivariance and symmetry

**Source:** Taco S. Cohen and Max Welling, **“Group Equivariant Convolutional Networks”** (2016). [Official PMLR paper page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/cohenc16.html); [arXiv:1602.07576](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.07576).

- **Exact mechanism:** Builds group equivariance into convolutional layers through transformed filters and parameter sharing.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Respecting known symmetries and avoiding arbitrary distinction between symmetry-related hypotheses is established machine-learning practice.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI's relevant issue is statistical identifiability *modulo automorphisms of the executable algebra or observation model*, not merely equivariant representation learning. The novelty, if any, lies in using that orbit structure as an explicit transfer gate for a surface symbol.

## 10. Proof-carrying code and contracts

### 10.1 Axiomatic program contracts

**Source:** C. A. R. Hoare, **“An Axiomatic Basis for Computer Programming”** (1969). [DOI: 10.1145/363235.363259](https://doi.org/10.1145/363235.363259).

- **Exact mechanism:** Specifies program behavior with preconditions and postconditions and derives correctness judgments using inference rules.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Executable components governed by formal behavioral contracts are foundational program logic.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI proposes to induce certain algebraic contracts from interaction and retain their verifier evidence; contract logic itself is not new.

### 10.2 Proof-carrying code

**Source:** George C. Necula, **“Proof-Carrying Code”** (1997). [DOI: 10.1145/263699.263712](https://doi.org/10.1145/263699.263712); [author publication page](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~necula/papers.html).

- **Exact mechanism:** An untrusted producer supplies code plus a proof that it satisfies a safety policy; the consumer checks the proof before execution.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Admission of a learned rule or constructor only with a checkable certificate is conceptually proof-carrying knowledge.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI would apply proof-carrying admission to induced semantic equations and surface-symbol contracts rather than received machine code. If OCI stores only successful tests, the analogy must not be upgraded to proof-carrying code.

## 11. Active automata and grammar learning

### 11.1 Membership/equivalence queries and distinguishing suffixes

**Source:** Dana Angluin, **“Learning Regular Sets from Queries and Counterexamples”** (1987). [DOI: 10.1016/0890-5401(87)90052-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/0890-5401(87)90052-6).

- **Exact mechanism:** The (L^*\) algorithm identifies a minimal deterministic automaton using membership queries and equivalence queries; suffix experiments distinguish residual states represented in an observation table.
- **Overlap with OCI:** A probe boundary signature that distinguishes candidate generators is an algebraic analogue of an observation row or distinguishing context. Active identification by verifier queries is not new in isolation.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI applies separating contexts to typed semantic generators, connects the result to novel surface-symbol transfer, and identifies only up to operadic/behavioral symmetries.

### 11.2 Active tree/grammar learning

**Source:** Benoît Barbot, Benedikt Bollig, Alain Finkel, Serge Haddad, Igor Khmelnitsky, Martin Leucker, Daniel Neider, Rajarshi Roy, and Lina Ye, **“Extracting Context-Free Grammars from Recurrent Neural Networks using Tree-Automata Learning and A* Search”** (2021). [Official PMLR paper page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v153/barbot21a.html).

- **Exact mechanism:** Actively learns visibly pushdown grammars via their relation to regular tree languages, using membership-style evidence and approximate equivalence checking by A* search and sampling.
- **Overlap with OCI:** Active learning over tree-structured composition and counterexample-driven refinement are close procedural prior art.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** The learned object is a grammar/surrogate automaton, not an executable colored-operad quotient jointly bound to surface symbols with proof-scoped equations.

### Relevance boundary

Active automata learning supplies the best established analogy for OCI's intervention signatures. The analogy also exposes a missing theorem obligation: OCI needs a finite candidate class or another learnability assumption, a teacher/verifier model, and a proof that its chosen context family separates generator orbits. Without these, “probe until identified” is a heuristic, not an identification guarantee.

## 12. Sheaf and local-to-global alternatives

**Source:** Samson Abramsky and Adam Brandenburger, **“The Sheaf-Theoretic Structure of Non-Locality and Contextuality”** (2011). [arXiv:1102.0264](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264); [DOI: 10.1088/1367-2630/13/11/113036](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/13/11/113036).

- **Exact mechanism:** Places compatible local observations over measurement contexts in a sheaf-theoretic framework; contextuality appears as an obstruction to a global section.
- **Overlap with OCI:** It formalizes when locally consistent probe evidence can or cannot be glued into a global latent assignment.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** It does not by itself provide generator substitution, executable operad semantics, equation completion, or canonical term normalization.

**Source:** Samson Abramsky and Mehrnoosh Sadrzadeh, **“Semantic Unification: A sheaf theoretic approach to natural language”** (2014). [arXiv:1403.3351](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.3351).

- **Exact mechanism:** Uses presheaves and gluing to combine local logical meanings of discourse fragments, including ambiguous/multivalued gluings.
- **Overlap with OCI:** It is direct primary prior art for local-to-global semantic consistency in language.
- **Narrow remaining delta:** OCI's central operation is typed substitution of constructors plus executable verification; sheaf gluing addresses compatibility across overlapping contexts instead.

## 14. Novelty determination

### 14.1 Claims OCI should not make

OCI should not claim novelty for:

1. **Operadic compositionality.** Applied operads already represent typed system composition and its algebras.
2. **Homomorphic OOD tree execution.** Initial-algebra and functorial semantics already give unique compositional extension once primitive meanings are fixed.
3. **Equations as contracts.** Foley et al. explicitly make this connection in an operadic setting.
4. **Canonical rewrite normalization.** Knuth–Bendix completion and confluence theory establish the relevant machinery and limitations.
5. **Learned rewrite rules from executables.** Ruler and Synantic are direct precedents.
6. **Categorical or tensor composition for semantics.** TPR and DisCoCat are established.
7. **Modular executable reasoning.** Neural module networks, visual programs, and program synthesis are established.
8. **Active identification by distinguishing tests.** Angluin-style query learning is direct prior art.
9. **Symmetry-aware learning.** Equivariance and identifiability modulo symmetries are established principles.
10. **Certified executable artifacts.** Hoare logic and proof-carrying code are foundational precedents.

### 14.2 Defensible provisional novelty delta

The remaining delta is the following *specific operational composition*:

- the hypothesis space is a free typed/colored operad of executable semantic constructors;
- semantics are authoritative executable algebras, not only learned vectors;
- candidate equations are stored as a proof-scoped operadic congruence and admitted only by a declared verifier/certificate policy;
- rewriting is promoted to canonical normalization only after termination and confluence evidence appropriate to the exact language;
- a novel surface token or phrase is not bound from correlation alone: the learner chooses typed intervention contexts and transfers only when the resulting boundary signature separates a generator orbit;
- the certified binding and equations persist and are reused on novel composition trees.

This is best described as **a provisional new synthesis of known ideas**, not a new operad, new rewriting theorem, or new active-learning paradigm.

### 14.3 Most serious novelty threat

Foley et al. (2021) plus Ruler (2021) plus Angluin (1987), read as a combined design recipe, already supply much of OCI:

\[
\text{operadic API/contracts}
+
\text{learned validated equations}
+
\text{active distinguishing contexts}.
\]

Bottman and Richardson (2026) additionally occupy the operads-for-compositional-reasoning niche. OCI remains distinguishable only if the experiment actually demonstrates the missing integration: active *surface-to-generator* induction modulo symmetry, exact evidence-authoritative equation admission, and transfer through a persistent quotient semantics. If those components are merely hand-authored or post hoc, the novelty delta collapses.

## 15. Research requirements implied by the audit

Before a positive OCI result can support even the provisional claim, the cycle should preregister:

1. **Algebra declaration.** Colors, generator profiles, semantic domains, observables, and whether the operad is symmetric, planar, or otherwise structured.
2. **Soundness scope.** For each verifier result, whether it is a proof, exhaustive finite-model check, solver certificate under assumptions, or bounded test.
3. **Congruence discipline.** Evidence that each learned equation is same-profile and closed under all legal operadic contexts.
4. **Normalization discipline.** A termination order and critical-pair/confluence analysis, or an explicit declaration that the system only maintains equivalence classes and does not have canonical normal forms.
5. **Identifiability theorem.** A finite candidate class and a proof that the allowed intervention contexts separate generator orbits—or a clear “not identifiable” outcome.
6. **No-leakage control.** Surface names must carry no generator identity through ordering, serialization, code identifiers, arity-specific accidents beyond declared type information, or evaluator metadata.
7. **Mechanism ablations.** Remove persistent contracts, learned equations, the verifier gate, active probe selection, and symmetry quotient one at a time.
8. **Prior-art baselines.** Include at least an explicit term interpreter without quotient learning, a Ruler/e-graph-style rule learner, a program-induction baseline, a neural-module baseline, and active versus random probes.
9. **Counterexamples.** Include observationally symmetric generators, non-separating probe families, non-terminating equation orientations, non-confluent critical pairs, and misspecified semantic generators.

The central falsifier should be strict: if a static explicit symbolic interpreter plus ordinary active query learning matches OCI after equalizing its access to types and verifier calls, the proposed operadic contract machinery has not earned a separate capability claim.

## 16. Search limits and candidate-rejection recommendation

### Candidate-rejection recommendation

**Reject the tensor-signature candidate as the primary formalism for the first OCI cycle.** Full tensor-product representations can preserve exact role–filler structure but incur dimensional growth and still do not supply executable contracts, congruence proofs, or canonical rewrite theory. Compressed schemes such as HRR reduce dimensional cost by accepting interference and approximate recovery; that is poorly matched to an experiment whose decisive claims concern exact equation admission and generator identification modulo a precisely declared symmetry. A tensor or learned embedding may later serve as an amortized retrieval index or query heuristic, but the explicit typed term, executable algebra, verifier record, and symbolic congruence should remain authoritative.

**Reject the sheaf/local-to-global candidate as the primary formalism for the first OCI cycle.** Sheaves are the right language when evidence is distributed over overlapping contexts and the scientific question is whether compatible local sections glue to a global assignment—or whether contextuality obstructs one. OCI's first-cycle problem is instead free typed substitution, executable homomorphic semantics, quotient equations, and active generator identification. Introducing a site, covers, restriction maps, stalks, and gluing conditions would add machinery without automatically providing those operations or a normalization procedure. Reconsider a sheaf layer only if later experiments make partial, overlapping, context-dependent contracts or inconsistent local conventions the central bottleneck.

### Explicit search limits

- The audit was performed on 2026-08-26 using bounded web searches across arXiv, ACM Digital Library/DOI pages, PMLR, ACL Anthology, CVF Open Access, Royal Society/NIST paper pages, publisher pages, and author repositories.
- Queries covered combinations of: operads, colored operads, compositional reasoning, functorial/algebraic semantics, Lawvere theories, system composition, equations as contracts, term rewriting, completion, equality saturation, rewrite-rule inference, tensor binding, categorical language semantics, neural modules, program induction, equivariance, proof-carrying code, active automata/tree learning, separating contexts, and sheaf/local-to-global semantics.
- The corpus emphasizes representative foundational and closest technical papers. It does not exhaust every paper citing those works, every categorical machine-learning preprint, dissertations, books, workshops, non-English literature, patents, or unpublished repositories.
- Full theorem-by-theorem inspection was not performed for every paper. Search indexing can miss papers whose title/abstract uses different terminology, especially work framed as universal algebra, clone theory, multicategories, institution learning, active algebra learning, inductive theorem discovery, or semantics-guided synthesis.
- The June 2026 operadic-reasoning papers materially narrow the claim and show that this is an active area. Later or poorly indexed work may narrow it further.
- No patentability, freedom-to-operate, or exhaustive novelty conclusion is intended.
- Therefore the valid conclusion is only: **no directly covering source was found in this bounded primary-source audit**. It is not a claim that no such source exists.

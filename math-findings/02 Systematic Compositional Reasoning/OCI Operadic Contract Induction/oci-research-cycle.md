# OCI — Operadic Contract Induction

**Research target:** systematic compositional generalization over novel symbol structures and rule compositions, with provable behavior on out-of-distribution compositions.  
**Cycle date:** 2026-08-26  
**Direct verdict:** **Mathematically sound under explicit closure, identification, and certificate assumptions; not yet an empirically validated Sentinel mechanism.** The exact OOD guarantee is a structural theorem about free typed composition, not a claim that arbitrary unseen meanings can be inferred without evidence. Most mathematical ingredients are established prior art. The provisional contribution is the specific learning loop that couples executable operadic semantics, verifier-scoped equation admission, active symmetry-aware symbol identification, and persistent transfer.

Scientific status used throughout:

- **REPRODUCED:** Lean 4 + Mathlib checked the binary-tree core; all local scripts reran successfully.
- **MEASURED:** bounded synthetic enumeration, graph experiments, symbolic checks, property tests, and resource checks were executed.
- **PROVED (paper):** the colored-operad extension, signature identifiability, and heterogeneous error bounds are proved below at paper level.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the full OCI learning loop will improve systematic transfer over matched explicit-symbolic and active-learning baselines.
- **SPECULATIVE:** relevance to broad AGI capabilities beyond the declared finite task family.

### A. Problem Target

#### Capability gap

A learner can fit familiar expressions while failing on a new tree assembled from familiar parts. Two failures are often conflated:

1. **Structural OOD:** the primitive meanings are known, but their composition tree, depth, rule order, or wiring pattern was absent from training.
2. **Semantic novelty:** a surface symbol denotes an unknown primitive or a familiar primitive under a new name.

The first admits an exact conditional solution: require the executor to be a homomorphic extension of primitive semantics over a typed free syntax. The second is impossible without identifying evidence; OCI therefore requires intervention signatures and permits identification only up to observational symmetry.

The target is not ordinary interpolation accuracy. It is the following auditable property:

> Once primitive constructors have been identified and certified, every legal unseen composition of those constructors is executed according to the same algebra, independent of whether that tree occurred in training. A fresh surface symbol is transferred only when its probe signature identifies a known generator or a declared symmetry orbit.

This addresses at least three Sentinel motivations—distribution-shift generalization, systematic compositional reasoning, and continual retention of learned contracts—and also supports intervention-based causal probing and verified tool composition. These are motivation mappings, not evidence that those broad capabilities have been achieved.

#### Formal task family and notation

Let:

- $C$ be a finite set of semantic types, called **colors**.
- $X_c$ be the semantic carrier or state space for color $c\in C$.
- $G(c_1,\ldots,c_k;c)$ be the finite set of primitive constructors with profile $(c_1,\ldots,c_k;c)$.
- $\mathcal F_C(G)$ be the free planar $C$-colored operad generated by $G$. Its elements are finite well-typed rooted trees. A symmetric version may be used only when input permutations are semantically legal.
- $\operatorname{End}_X(c_1,\ldots,c_k;c)=\operatorname{Hom}(X_{c_1}\times\cdots\times X_{c_k},X_c)$.
- $A:\mathcal F_C(G)\to\operatorname{End}_X$ be the authoritative executable algebra induced by primitive interpretations $A_g$.
- $S_{\mathrm{tr}}\subset\mathcal F_C(G)$ be the structural support observed during training.
- $S_{\mathrm{ood}}\subset\mathcal F_C(G)\setminus S_{\mathrm{tr}}$ be held-out trees containing known generator profiles in unseen combinations, shapes, depths, or rewrite paths.
- $\Lambda$ be a surface-symbol set and $\pi:\Lambda\rightharpoonup G$ a persistent but partially unknown symbol-to-generator binding.
- $V_j$ be the verifier observable for intervention context $q_j$, such as exact output, trace projection, or postcondition vector.

For a surface term $s$, a structural parser supplies a typed syntax tree $T(s)$. OCI does not assume that the parser is automatically correct. Its structural target is

\[
\widehat A\bigl(\widehat\pi(T(s))\bigr)
=
A\bigl(\pi(T(s))\bigr)
\quad\text{for }\pi(T(s))\in S_{\mathrm{ood}},
\]

conditional on correct typing, adequate primitive coverage, identifying evidence for $\widehat\pi$, and certificate-valid primitive semantics.

**Impossibility boundary.** If a fresh symbol could denote either $g$ or $h$, all available observations have the same distribution under both hypotheses, and the required outputs differ somewhere, no learner can determine which world holds. Under an equal prior, every decision rule has worst-case error at least $1/2$. OCI therefore makes no zero-shot guarantee for a genuinely new semantic primitive with no distinguishing evidence.

### B. New Mathematical Construct

#### Candidate generation and rejection

Three distinct candidates were developed before selection.

| Candidate | Core representation | Conditional guarantee | Principal defect | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Operadic Contract Induction (OCI)** | Free colored-operad terms, executable algebra, proof-scoped quotient equations, intervention signatures | Exact extension to every well-typed unseen tree; explicit identifiability and error bounds | Requires correct primitives, compositional closure, and difficult certificates | **Survivor** |
| **Interventional Tensor-Signature Factorization** | Role–filler response tensors with multilinear contraction | OOD recovery under rank, multilinearity, and decomposition-uniqueness assumptions | Gauge ambiguity, dimensional growth or compression interference, no native rewrite proof discipline | Rejected as authority layer |
| **Sheaf-Coherent Semantic Patching** | Local semantic sections over overlapping contexts with gluing | Global meaning when compatibility and unique-gluing conditions hold | Local consistency may have no or many global sections; does not supply substitution or canonical execution | Rejected for first cycle |

The tensor candidate remains plausible as an amortized retrieval index. The sheaf candidate becomes relevant if context-dependent partial contracts—not executable substitution—become the central bottleneck. Neither is the correct authoritative representation for this cycle.

#### Definition 1: free executable composition space

The syntax space is $\mathcal F_C(G)$, not a vector space of whole expressions. Each constructor $g\in G(c_1,\ldots,c_k;c)$ has an executable interpretation

\[
A_g:X_{c_1}\times\cdots\times X_{c_k}\to X_c.
\]

The unique structural evaluator is defined recursively:

\[
\llbracket g(t_1,\ldots,t_k)\rrbracket_A
=
A_g\bigl(\llbracket t_1\rrbracket_A,\ldots,
          \llbracket t_k\rrbracket_A\bigr).
\]

The free syntax deliberately contains more trees than training. OOD composition is therefore represented directly rather than approximated as proximity to a training expression.

#### Definition 2: boundary contract

Each generator is stored with a proof-scoped contract

\[
\kappa_g=
(\tau_g,P_g,Q_g,\widehat A_g,L_g,\varepsilon_g,\chi_g,\Sigma_g),
\]

where:

- $\tau_g=(c_1,\ldots,c_k;c)$ is the exact profile;
- $P_g\subseteq\prod_i X_{c_i}$ is the admitted input domain;
- $Q_g\subseteq X_c$ is the postcondition or output invariant;
- $\widehat A_g$ is the current executor;
- $L_g=(L_{g,1},\ldots,L_{g,k})$ is a coordinate sensitivity certificate;
- $\varepsilon_g$ bounds local implementation error against the declared reference semantics on $P_g$, when such a reference is available;
- $\chi_g$ records certificate kind, assumptions, proof hash, verifier version, and domain scope;
- $\Sigma_g$ stores intervention observations used to bind surface symbols.

Required contract conditions are

\[
x\in P_g\Longrightarrow A_g(x)\in Q_g,
\]

and, for product metrics,

\[
d_c(A_g(x),A_g(y))
\le
\sum_{i=1}^{k}L_{g,i}
d_{c_i}(x_i,y_i).
\]

Composition is admitted only when every child postcondition entails the parent-slot precondition. This turns type checking into a necessary but not sufficient boundary check.

#### Definition 3: proof-scoped operadic quotient

A candidate relation is a same-profile pair $r=(\ell,rhs)$ in $\mathcal F_C(G)$. It becomes a **certified equation** only with a scoped witness

\[
\chi_r:
\forall x\in P_r,
\quad
\llbracket\ell\rrbracket_A(x)
=
\llbracket rhs\rrbracket_A(x).
\]

Let $E$ be the admitted equations and $\equiv_E$ their compatible operadic congruence. The learned semantic syntax is

\[
\mathcal O_E=\mathcal F_C(G)/{\equiv_E}.
\]

If equations can be oriented into a terminating and confluent rewrite system $R$, OCI may use a canonical normalizer $N_R$. Otherwise it stores equivalence in an e-graph or congruence ledger and must not claim a unique normal form.

Random testing is not a universal certificate. OCI distinguishes:

- a Lean/Coq/Isabelle or sound proof-kernel certificate;
- an SMT certificate under an explicit decidable theory;
- exhaustive finite-model verification, valid only for that finite model;
- bounded testing, which remains **HYPOTHESIS** evidence and cannot enter the universal equation set.

#### Definition 4: intervention boundary signature

For a typed one-hole context $q_j[-]$, define

\[
\sigma_Q(g)
=
\bigl(V_1(\llbracket q_1[g]\rrbracket_A),\ldots,
      V_m(\llbracket q_m[g]\rrbracket_A)\bigr).
\]

Two generators are observationally equivalent under $Q$ when

\[
g\sim_Q h
\iff
\sigma_Q(g)=\sigma_Q(h).
\]

Let

\[
\Gamma_Q=
\{\gamma\in\operatorname{Sym}(G_\tau):
\sigma_Q(\gamma g)=\sigma_Q(g)\ \forall g\}
\]

be the observation-preserving automorphism group for a profile $\tau$. A fresh surface symbol may be bound exactly only to a singleton $\sim_Q$-class; otherwise OCI binds to the orbit/class, asks another question, creates an unresolved primitive, or abstains.

The active query is

\[
q^*
=
\arg\max_{q\in\mathcal Q}
\frac{I(G;Y_q\mid H)}{c(q)},
\]

where $H$ is accumulated contract evidence. No greedy approximation guarantee is claimed without proving the required conditional-independence or adaptive-submodularity assumptions.

#### Definition 5: constrained learning objective

OCI treats verification as a hard admission boundary, not a soft regularizer. A finite implementation can optimize

\[
\begin{aligned}
\min_{\theta,\pi,E}\quad
&\sum_{(s,x,y)\in D}
  \ell\bigl(\llbracket \pi(T(s))\rrbracket_{\widehat A_\theta}(x),y\bigr)
  +\beta\,\mathrm{DL}(G,E,\pi,\theta)\\
&+\lambda
  \sum_g\left[\sum_iL_{g,i}-\rho_g\right]_+^2
\end{aligned}
\]

subject to:

\[
\operatorname{Typed}(T(s)),\quad
\operatorname{Closed}(\{\kappa_g\}),\quad
\operatorname{Certified}(e)\ \forall e\in E,
\]

and, when canonical normalization is enabled,

\[
\operatorname{Terminating}(R)
\land
\operatorname{Confluent}(R).
\]

The description-length term penalizes redundant primitives, relations, and symbol bindings. It is a computable two-part code, not Kolmogorov complexity.

#### Counterexample-guided quotient refinement

The update rule is structural:

1. Localize a verifier counterexample to a primitive contract, surface binding, composition boundary, or proposed equation.
2. Split an observational class when a new context distinguishes its members.
3. Update $\widehat A_g$ only within its declared versioned domain; retain contradictory evidence.
4. Propose an equation only between same-profile terms.
5. Admit it to $E$ only after a declared certificate succeeds.
6. Orient it only under a well-founded decrease; check critical pairs. If completion fails, retain the equality without claiming canonical normalization.
7. Persist the new contract and its evidence for later trees.

#### Intuition

- Novel trees are native elements of a free typed syntax rather than extrapolated points in an embedding space.
- Primitive meanings are the only semantic degrees of freedom; the tree evaluator is forced by the algebra laws.
- New names are treated as latent bindings and transferred only after intervention evidence separates their possible roles.
- Learned equations reduce redundant computation, but only proof-scoped equations can change authoritative execution.
- Counterexamples refine the quotient and symbol partition instead of globally overwriting unrelated knowledge.
- Exactness and approximation are separated: exact structural soundness is possible, while numerical error is transported through explicit sensitivity contracts.

### C. Theoretical Results

#### Theorem 1 — exact OOD operadic extension and certified-rewrite soundness

Let $\mathcal F_C(G)$ be the free $C$-colored operad on $G$. Let $A$ and $\widehat A$ be two algebras into $\operatorname{End}_X$ that agree on every generator and identity. Then

\[
\widehat A(t)=A(t)
\quad\text{for every }t\in\mathcal F_C(G),
\]

including every $t\notin S_{\mathrm{tr}}$.

Further, let $R$ be a context-closed rewrite relation such that every root rule $\ell\to r$ is semantically sound:

\[
A(\ell)=A(r).
\]

If $t\to_R^*u$, then $A(t)=A(u)$. Therefore any normalizer $N$ satisfying $t\to_R^*N(t)$ is semantics-preserving. Unique canonical forms require the additional, separate assumptions of termination and confluence.

**Proof.** Every term is either an identity/generator or an operadic graft $g(t_1,\ldots,t_k)$. Agreement is immediate on generators and identities. Assume inductively that $\widehat A(t_i)=A(t_i)$ for every child. Because both maps preserve operadic substitution,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat A(g(t_1,\ldots,t_k))
&=\widehat A_g(\widehat A(t_1),\ldots,\widehat A(t_k))\\
&=A_g(A(t_1),\ldots,A(t_k))\\
&=A(g(t_1,\ldots,t_k)).
\end{aligned}
\]

The proof does not inspect membership in $S_{\mathrm{tr}}$; therefore it applies to held-out structures.

For rewriting, a sound root equation remains sound in any one-hole context because equal child denotations are passed as equal arguments to the same parent operation. Induction on context depth proves one-step context closure. Induction on the finite reflexive-transitive rewrite chain gives $A(t)=A(u)$. Termination or confluence was not used, so neither may be inferred from semantic preservation. ∎

**Status.** The result is classical initial/free-algebra semantics, not a novel theorem. The binary-tree subcase, the outside-training corollary, one-step context closure, finite rewrite-chain preservation, and normalizer soundness were **REPRODUCED** in Lean 4 + Mathlib.

#### Theorem 2 — necessary and sufficient symbol identifiability

Fix a profile $\tau$, a finite candidate set $G_\tau$, and intervention family $Q$. A generator $g\in G_\tau$ is exactly identifiable from $\sigma_Q(g)$ if and only if its equivalence class

\[
[g]_Q=\{h:\sigma_Q(h)=\sigma_Q(g)\}
\]

is a singleton. Every generator is identifiable if and only if $\sigma_Q:G_\tau\to\prod_jY_j$ is injective.

If $\sigma_Q$ is not injective, no learner using only the $Q$-observations can distinguish all generators. If two indistinguishable hypotheses have equal prior probability and require different actions, every decision rule has Bayes error at least $1/2$ on that pair. If each query has at most $M$ possible outputs, identifying $k$ distinct classes requires

\[
m\ge\left\lceil\log_M k\right\rceil.
\]

When an observation-preserving automorphism group acts on $G_\tau$, identification is possible only up to its observational orbit unless extra contexts break that symmetry.

**Proof.** If $\sigma_Q$ is injective, equal observed signatures imply equal generators, so lookup is unique. Conversely, if it is not injective, choose $g\ne h$ with the same signature. The observation supplied to any learner is identical in both worlds, while the correct labels differ; exact identification in both worlds is impossible. With equal priors, assigning either label is wrong in one of the two equiprobable worlds, giving error at least $1/2$. Finally, $m$ queries with at most $M$ outcomes yield no more than $M^m$ distinct signatures. Injectivity over $k$ classes requires $M^m\ge k$, which gives the bound. Observation-preserving automorphisms map hypotheses without changing evidence, so only the orbit can be identified. ∎

#### Corollary 2.1 — noisy binary probe sample complexity

Suppose each of $m$ separating binary signature bits for each of $k$ generators is observed $r$ times through independent bit-flip noise $\eta<1/2$, and majority vote is used. Hoeffding's inequality and a union bound give

\[
\Pr[\text{any recovered bit is wrong}]
\le
km\exp\bigl(-2r(1/2-\eta)^2\bigr).
\]

Thus a sufficient condition for total signature error at most $\delta$ is

\[
r\ge
\frac{\log(km/\delta)}{2(1/2-\eta)^2}.
\]

This is a sufficient finite-class bound, not a claim for dependent noise or adaptive distribution shift.

#### Lemma 1 — heterogeneous compositional error transport

Assume each authoritative operation obeys

\[
d(A_g(x),A_g(y))\le\sum_iL_{g,i}d(x_i,y_i),
\]

and each approximate operation obeys, on the entire reached contract domain,

\[
d(\widehat A_g(x),A_g(x))\le\varepsilon_g.
\]

Let $E(t)=d(\llbracket t\rrbracket_{\widehat A},\llbracket t\rrbracket_A)$. Then

\[
E(g(t_1,\ldots,t_k))
\le
\varepsilon_g+\sum_iL_{g,i}E(t_i).
\]

Equivalently,

\[
E(t)
\le
\sum_{v\in\operatorname{Int}(t)}
\varepsilon_v
\prod_{e\in\operatorname{path}(v\to root)}L_e
+
\sum_{\ell\in\operatorname{Leaf}(t)}
E(\ell)
\prod_{e\in\operatorname{path}(\ell\to root)}L_e,
\]

where the product uses the parent sensitivity for the traversed child slot; the empty product is one.

**Proof.** Insert and subtract $A_g(\widehat x_1,\ldots,\widehat x_k)$, apply the triangle inequality, then apply the local implementation bound and coordinate Lipschitz bound. Recursively substitute the child inequalities. ∎

#### Corollary 1.1 — stability by aggregate sensitivity

If $\sum_iL_{g,i}\le\rho$ for every reachable node, $\varepsilon_g\le\bar\varepsilon$, all leaves have error at most $E_0$, and tree depth is $d$, then

\[
E_d\le\rho^dE_0+\bar\varepsilon\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\rho^j.
\]

Therefore:

\[
E_d\le
\begin{cases}
\rho^dE_0+\bar\varepsilon\dfrac{1-\rho^d}{1-\rho},&0\le\rho<1,\\[6pt]
E_0+d\bar\varepsilon,&\rho=1,\\[4pt]
\rho^dE_0+\bar\varepsilon\dfrac{\rho^d-1}{\rho-1},&\rho>1.
\end{cases}
\]

So exact structural generalization does not imply numerical stability. Expansive contracts may amplify small primitive error exponentially with depth.

#### Lemma 2 — conditional unique normal forms

If the oriented relation $R$ is terminating and locally confluent on the declared typed language, then it is confluent and every term has a unique $R$-normal form. This is an application of established rewriting theory; it was **not** independently mechanized in this cycle. OCI must store termination and confluence obligations separately from semantic rule soundness.

#### Assumption stress test

| Assumption | Why needed | Counterexample if removed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Correct typed tree | The algebra executes syntax, not raw language | Parser swaps argument roles | **UNKNOWN** in natural language |
| Primitive coverage | Free extension uses known generators | Truly new primitive has no semantics | Necessary |
| Compositional closure | Parent depends only on declared child interfaces | Hidden global state changes meaning | Necessary |
| Contract-domain closure | Error and safety bounds hold only on $P_g$ | OOD child output leaves parent domain | Necessary |
| Correct primitive semantics | Structural induction starts at generators | One wrong primitive corrupts all containing trees | Necessary |
| Separating contexts | Fresh names need identifying evidence | Two generators share every probe signature | Necessary and sufficient in finite model |
| Sound equation certificates | Rewrites alter authoritative execution | Near-law passes finite samples but fails elsewhere | Necessary |
| Termination + confluence | Canonicalization needs unique normal forms | Looping rules or divergent critical pair | Necessary for canonical claim |
| Stable sensitivities | Numerical errors must not explode | $\rho>1$ at depth | Sufficient, not always necessary |

The strongest boundary is primitive adequacy. OCI proves systematic recombination of identified constructors; it does not prove autonomous discovery of an unrestricted semantic ontology.

#### Claims formalized first

The executable file `formal/OCI.lean` checks the finite binary-tree core:

#### Executable Lean theorem signatures

```lean
theorem fold_unique
    (atomSem : Atom → X) (nodeSem : Generator → X → X → X)
    (candidate : BTerm Generator Atom → X)
    (hatom : ∀ leaf, candidate (.atom leaf) = atomSem leaf)
    (hnode : ∀ generator left right,
      candidate (.node generator left right) =
        nodeSem generator (candidate left) (candidate right)) :
    ∀ term, candidate term = eval atomSem nodeSem term

theorem exact_outside_training
    ...
    (training : Set (BTerm Generator Atom))
    (term : BTerm Generator Atom)
    (_hood : term ∉ training) :
    candidate term = eval atomSem nodeSem term

theorem step_preserves_eval
    ...
    (hsound : ∀ rule ∈ rules,
      eval atomSem nodeSem rule.lhs = eval atomSem nodeSem rule.rhs) :
    ∀ {before after}, Step rules before after →
      eval atomSem nodeSem before = eval atomSem nodeSem after

theorem certified_normalizer_sound
    ...
    (hreduces : ∀ term,
      Relation.ReflTransGen (Step rules) term (normalize term)) :
    eval atomSem nodeSem (normalize term) = eval atomSem nodeSem term

theorem depthError_eq_sum (rho epsilon initial : ℝ) (n : ℕ) :
    depthError rho epsilon initial n =
      rho ^ n * initial + epsilon * ∑ i ∈ Finset.range n, rho ^ i
```

### E. Mechanism/Architecture Instantiation

#### Computational mechanism

```text
surface expression / symbolic structure
            │
            ▼
typed parser and profile checker
            │
            ▼
surface-binding posterior ── active intervention queries
            │                         │
            └──────── evidence ◄──────┘
            │
            ▼
free colored-operad term
            │
            ▼
contract-closure gate
            │
            ├─ unresolved/unsafe ──► ask, expand, or abstain
            ▼
proof-scoped quotient / e-graph
            │
            ├─ convergent R ──► certified normal form
            └─ otherwise ─────► original/equivalent term, no canonical claim
            │
            ▼
executable algebra
            │
            ▼
authoritative verifier and counterexample localizer
            │
            ▼
versioned contract, equation, and symbol-binding ledger
```

This can sit beside Sentinel's existing executable program hypotheses and evidence-authoritative policy. It does not require replacing the current verifier, version-space states, or open-world abstention mechanism.

#### Pseudocode

```text
procedure OCI_EXECUTE(surface_term s, evidence H, query_budget B):
    ast ← PARSE_TYPED(s)
    if ast is ill_typed:
        return ABSTAIN("ill_typed_or_unknown_construction")

    for each unresolved surface constructor λ in ast:
        candidates ← GENERATORS_WITH_PROFILE(type_of(λ)) ∪ {OTHER}
        posterior ← UPDATE_FROM_STORED_SIGNATURES(candidates, H)

        while posterior is not concentrated on one observational orbit
              and B > 0:
            q ← argmax_q mutual_information(candidates; answer(q) | H) / cost(q)
            y ← ASK_OR_INTERVENE(q)
            H ← H ∪ {(q, y)}
            posterior ← BAYES_UPDATE(posterior, q, y)
            B ← B - 1

        if mass(OTHER) is high or no in-class candidate matches:
            return EXPAND_OR_ABSTAIN("unknown_primitive")
        if more than one behaviorally distinct orbit remains:
            return ABSTAIN("non_identifiable_surface_binding")
        bind λ to the surviving orbit with evidence provenance

    term ← INSTANTIATE_OPERAD_TERM(ast, bindings)
    if not CONTRACT_CLOSED(term):
        return ABSTAIN("contract_boundary_violation")

    if rewrite_system.is_proved_terminating_and_confluent:
        executable_term ← NORMALIZE(term)
    else:
        executable_term ← SAFE_REPRESENTATIVE(term)  // no canonical claim

    output, trace ← EXECUTABLE_ALGEBRA(executable_term)
    check ← AUTHORITATIVE_VERIFY(term, output, trace)

    if check is counterexample:
        LOCALIZE_AND_REFINE(check, bindings, contracts, equation_ledger)
        return ABSTAIN_OR_RETRY(check)

    PERSIST_VERSIONED_EVIDENCE(bindings, contracts, check)
    return output

procedure OCI_PROPOSE_EQUATION(lhs, rhs):
    if profile(lhs) ≠ profile(rhs):
        reject
    certificate ← PROVE_OR_EXHAUSTIVELY_CHECK(lhs = rhs, declared_domain)
    if certificate is only sampled evidence:
        store as HYPOTHESIS; do not alter authoritative quotient
    else:
        add equation and certificate scope to congruence ledger
        if an orientation strictly decreases a well-founded ranking:
            attempt critical-pair completion
        if termination or confluence is not established:
            retain equality in e-graph; disable canonical-normal-form claim
```

#### Complexity analysis

Let $n$ be tree size, $h$ depth, $g_\tau=|G_\tau|$, $m$ probes, $r=|R|$, $K(t)$ the number of rewrite steps taken, and $C_A$ the average primitive execution cost.

| Operation | Time | Memory | Boundary |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Type/profile check | $O(n)$ | $O(h)$ streaming | Assumes explicit typed AST |
| Exact signature matching | $O(g_\tau m)$ per fresh symbol | $O(g_\tau m)$ | Can identify only observed classes |
| Naive information-gain query scan | $O(|\mathcal Q|g_\tau|Y|)$ | $O(g_\tau|Y|)$ | Larger mapping posteriors may be factorial |
| Tree execution | $O(nC_A)$ | $O(h\,\dim X)$ DFS | Parallel tree evaluation can trade memory for depth |
| Naive rewriting | $O(K(t)nr)$ | $O(n+|E|)$ | $K$ can be unbounded without termination |
| E-graph saturation | Workload-dependent, often superlinear | Can grow rapidly | No general polynomial bound |
| Critical-pair generation | $O(r^2U)$ before recursive completion | Potentially large | Completion may diverge |
| Contract ledger | $O(|G|+|E|+|H|)$ records | Same | Requires evidence compaction/versioning |

The full word problem for arbitrary finitely presented algebras is not generally decidable. OCI therefore cannot promise successful completion or canonical normalization for every induced theory.

4. **False rewrite boundary.** The operation $x\star y=x+y+\eta x^2y$ was tested against an invalid associativity rule across perturbation and input-scale grids.

These tasks isolate theorem boundaries. They do not contain natural language, a learned parser, high-dimensional world states, or the integrated Sentinel architecture.

#### Target decisive experiment: OCI-1

Freeze a finite typed algebra before hidden testing:

- 4–6 colors;
- 12–24 primitive constructors with mixed arities;
- exact finite domains for a subset and continuous contractive domains for another subset;
- 3–8 valid equations with formal or exhaustive certificates;
- near-laws that pass shallow probes but fail held-out counterexamples;
- hidden surface permutations plus at least one observational automorphism;
- train trees of depth $\le3$, test trees of depth 4–10;
- independent holdouts for tree shapes, generator combinations, surface names, equation paths, and probe contexts;
- at least 20 frozen algebras and four untouched seeds.

Required arms:

1. Flat exact-expression memorizer.
2. Static typed interpreter with oracle surface bindings.
3. Static typed interpreter with ordinary active binding but no quotient learning.
4. Tensor/HRR signature model.
5. Neural module or neural interpreter baseline.
6. Ruler/e-graph-style equation learner with matched verifier access.
7. OCI without active probes.
8. OCI without persistent bindings.
9. OCI without equation induction.
10. OCI with bounded tests wrongly treated as certificates, as an intentionally unsafe negative control.
11. Full OCI.
12. Oracle primitive and oracle-equation ceilings.

#### Conditions expected to fail

- Nonseparating context families: binding remains at an equivalence class.
- An unseen semantic primitive outside $G$: `OTHER`, expansion, or abstention is required.
- Hidden global state or context-sensitive primitives: free-operad compositionality is misspecified.
- A child output outside the parent contract domain: error and safety bounds are invalid.
- $\rho>1$ at sustained depth: approximation error can grow exponentially.
- Sample-tested equations promoted as universal: adversarial contexts break rewrites.
- Nonterminating orientations or unresolved critical pairs: canonical normalization must be disabled.

### G. Comparison to Existing Methods

#### Closest primary prior art

The complete bounded audit is in `prior-art-audit.md`. The most important collisions are:

- [Lawvere, *Functorial Semantics of Algebraic Theories*](https://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/reprints/articles/5/tr5abs.html) and [Goguen et al., initial-algebra semantics](https://doi.org/10.1145/321992.321997): unique homomorphic extension from syntax to semantics is established mathematics.
- [Spivak, wiring-diagram operads](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0297) and [Vagner, Spivak, and Lerman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1598): operads already provide typed executable system composition.
- [Foley et al.](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2021.0099): the closest collision; operadic system design already includes equations as API-like contracts and normal-form computations.
- [Bottman and Richardson (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13634): operads have now been applied directly to compositional reasoning in language models.
- [Knuth and Bendix](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-08-012975-4.50028-X), [Huet](https://doi.org/10.1145/322217.322230), and [egg](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03082): completion, confluence, congruence maintenance, and equality saturation are established.
- [Nandi et al., Ruler](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10436): inferring rewrite rules from executable semantics with domain-specific validation is direct prior art.
- [Angluin](https://doi.org/10.1016/0890-5401(87)90052-6): active identification with distinguishing contexts is established query-learning machinery.
- [Smolensky](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(90)90007-M), [Coecke, Sadrzadeh, and Clark](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.4394), and [Andreas et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.02799): tensor binding, categorical language composition, and modular executable trees are established alternatives.
- [SCAN](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00350), [COGS](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.731/), and [CFQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09713): established diagnostics for held-out composition, not mechanisms proving OCI's loop.
- [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381): persistent reusable program abstractions and transfer are prior art.
- [Hoare](https://doi.org/10.1145/363235.363259) and [Necula](https://doi.org/10.1145/263699.263712): contracts and proof-carrying executable artifacts are foundational precedents.

#### Expressivity, efficiency, and robustness delta

- **Expressivity:** a colored operad natively represents variable-arity typed trees and substitution. It does not automatically represent unrestricted graph sharing, recursion, hidden global state, or context-sensitive meaning; those require richer operads, traced/recursive structure, or explicit state.
- **Efficiency:** once bindings are known, evaluation is linear in tree size. Equation completion and e-graph saturation can dominate and have no universal tractable bound.
- **Robustness:** proof-scoped equations prevent tested near-laws from silently becoming universal rewrites. This helps only if the certificate kernel and declared domain are adequate.
- **OOD delta:** exact behavior applies to new combinations of known primitives. It does not apply to unknown primitive semantics, parser errors, or noncompositional environments.

#### Novelty delta

No individual mathematical component is new. The bounded search found no single source combining all of:

1. executable colored-operad semantics;
2. persistent, verifier-gated quotient induction;
3. canonicalization only after termination/confluence obligations;
4. active surface-symbol identification by typed intervention signatures;
5. identification only up to observational automorphism; and
6. reuse of those proof-scoped bindings and equations on later unseen trees.

Accordingly, novelty is a **HYPOTHESIS about the integration**, not a theorem or patent conclusion. Foley et al. + Ruler + Angluin already form a strong combined recipe, and the 2026 operadic-reasoning papers further narrow the claim. If the active binding, proof gate, and persistent transfer are hand-authored or do not contribute under ablation, the novelty claim collapses.

### H. Failure Modes & Boundary Conditions

#### Strongest objections first

1. **The hard problem may have been moved into primitive identification and parsing.** The OOD theorem is exact only after the correct tree and generator semantics are available. This is a real limitation, not a rebutted concern.
2. **The core theorem is classical.** Homomorphic extension from generators is initial-algebra semantics. OCI's research value must come from the learned, verified integration and measured transfer, not renaming a universal property.
3. **Finite testing is not proof.** A learned near-law can pass every development probe and fail catastrophically under a new context. Only scoped proof or exhaustive finite verification may alter authoritative rewriting.
4. **Completion can fail.** Termination, confluence, and finite canonical presentations are not guaranteed. E-graphs avoid premature orientation but may grow explosively.
5. **Compositionality may be false.** Hidden state, resource interactions, side effects, aliasing, or contextual semantics can make local contracts insufficient.
6. **Types can leak answers.** If hidden surface symbols have unique arities, ordering, identifiers, or serialization artifacts, apparent induction may be authored inversion.
7. **Symmetry can be fundamental.** No query policy can distinguish hypotheses in the same observational orbit without new observables.
8. **Exact symbolic gates can be brittle under noise.** A probabilistic contract posterior and calibrated abstention are required outside exact finite domains.
9. **Persistent false contracts can poison many future compositions.** Versioning, rollback, and contradiction retention are mandatory.
10. **The closest prior-art composition may already subsume the design.** A matched Ruler/e-graph plus ordinary active learner could erase OCI's claimed contribution.

#### Adversarial cases

- Two generators differ only outside every allowed probe domain.
- A surface name changes meaning by parent context, violating a single persistent binding.
- A malicious generator matches all current postconditions but triggers a side effect not included in $V$.
- A rewrite pair is extensionally equal on outputs but differs in continuation-relevant state or cost.
- Rules $a\to b$ and $b\to a$ both receive local support, producing a loop.
- Two sound rules form an unresolved critical pair with different irreducible terms.
- A contractive operation is followed by an expansive one, making aggregate path sensitivity exceed one.
- The semantic grammar omits the true constructor, but nearest-signature matching would force an in-class singleton without `OTHER`.

#### Identifiability issues

- Signature injectivity is relative to the candidate class, context family, and observable. Expanding any of these can invalidate a previous uniqueness claim.
- Identification up to a surface permutation is insufficient if downstream continuation distinguishes internal state not observed by the verifier.
- Adaptive queries can overfit a misspecified candidate pool; posterior concentration is not model adequacy.
- The lower bound $m\ge\lceil\log_Mk\rceil$ is only a counting bound. Structural constraints can require many more contexts.

#### Optimization pathologies

- The description-length objective may merge distinct primitives that agree on current probes.
- A sensitivity penalty can encourage artificially contractive but semantically biased executors.
- Jointly learning parsing, bindings, primitive functions, and equations produces permutation symmetries and local optima.
- E-graph extraction cost may select fast but contract-domain-fragile representatives.
- Counterexample localization can blame a parent contract when the true error is a child binding.

#### Disconfirmation criteria

OCI should be rejected or substantially revised if any central result persists after one of these falsifiers:

- A static typed interpreter plus matched active query learning equals full OCI on transfer, regret, and query count.
- Learned equations do not improve accuracy, runtime, query efficiency, or description length after controlling for primitive coverage.
- Active interventions do not beat random interventions under matched budgets.
- Binding gains persist after the contract ledger is shuffled or reset, indicating task repetition or leakage rather than persistent convention learning.
- False universal equations enter the authoritative quotient above the preregistered rate.
- The measured error exceeds its declared path-sum certificate on in-domain inputs.
- Performance collapses on held-out tree shapes after conditioning on correct primitive bindings.
- Oracle parsing fixes most failures, showing that OCI did not address the actual bottleneck.
- Hidden generators remain in nontrivial observational classes under the allowed query family.
- Final-test failures lead to edits of the generator family, rule set, or probe generator.

### I. Iteration Step

#### Weakest assumption to refine

The weakest assumption is that a correct finite primitive inventory and typed parse already exist. This makes the exact OOD result valuable but narrow. The next cycle must permit an unknown primitive and uncertainty over parse structure without allowing either uncertainty to contaminate certified equations.

#### Next-generation variant

Develop **POCI — Posterior Operadic Contract Induction**:

- maintain a posterior over typed parse trees, generator bindings, and `OTHER` expansions;
- preserve an exact proof kernel for admitted equations while using probabilistic evidence only for proposal and query selection;
- keep ambiguous terms as sets/e-classes rather than selecting a premature normal form;
- choose interventions that distinguish both parse and generator orbits;
- use versioned domain-indexed contracts so a rule proved on one domain cannot silently generalize to another;
- add a rollback theorem: retracting one unsound certificate removes every dependent rewrite and derived binding without changing independent contracts.

The cheapest decisive next experiment is OCI-1 from Section F. Its primary falsifier is simple: after equalizing type information and verifier calls, full OCI must beat a static typed interpreter plus ordinary active binding on later unseen trees, and that advantage must disappear when persistent proof-scoped contracts are removed. Until that test passes, OCI remains a mathematically coherent, mechanically checked proposal—not a demonstrated new capability.
